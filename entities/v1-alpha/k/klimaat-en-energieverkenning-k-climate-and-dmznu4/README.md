# Climate and Energy Outlook (KEV) 2020 PBL

> These files concern the table annex and the data of the figures of the Climate and Energy Outlook (KEV) 2020 published on 30 October 2020. For a description of the backgrounds of the data in this report, reference is therefore made to the main report. The Climate and Energy Outlook (KEV) has been given an explicit role in the Climate Act to monitor the progress of climate policy. Once a year, the KEV should report in a clear and comprehensive manner on the full breadth of the climate and energy policy pursued and its expected effects. The KEV 2020 therefore provides insight into the developments of Dutch greenhouse gas emissions in the past and up to 2030. The emissions show a decrease, but not enough to reach the target of a 49% reduction by 2030 compared to 1990. The KEV also paints an integral picture of developments in energy supply and energy consumption, but also of other activities that lead to greenhouse gas emissions, such as in agriculture and land use. The KEV also makes the connections between autonomous developments, developments abroad and the Netherlands.
