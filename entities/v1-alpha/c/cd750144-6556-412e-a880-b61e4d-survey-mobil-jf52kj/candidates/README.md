# Survey Mobility station Ulm

> <p>The dataset contains survey results on the topic &quot;What offer would you use and try out here at the upcoming mobility station?&quot; . In the survey from 2.7.2021 to 23.7.2021, the Digital Agenda Office wanted to know in advance in the funding project Zukunftskommune@bw which of the upcoming offers at the prototype of the mobility station on Eselsberg, which was opened on 24.7.2021, is more interesting for the citizenry. 184 times the buttons were pressed by two survey posters, which were attached to lanterns near the upcoming mobility station.</p><p>At the same time there were 2 <a href="https://www.zukunftsstadt-ulm.de/eselsberg/survey posters">survey posters with pressure buttons</a>:</p><ul><li> a survey poster (105 responses) <a href="https://goo.gl/maps/529WCDkM7xxz4q1B9">on the footpath in the student residence</a><li> a survey poster (79 responses) <a href="https://goo.gl/maps/1zKp5aByqhibSzE7">on the footpath along the Eselssteige</a><li/<pul>>Diep Press buttons were occupied with these 4 answer options:</p><ul><li> eScooter (total: 28)</li><li> eLastenrad (total: 40)</li><li> e-car (total: 34)</li><li> bike service station (total: 82)</li></ul><p>The survey is anonymous and is carried out by hanging the survey poster so that passers-by can vote on different issues at the touch of a button. The data is transmitted every minute via LoRaWAN, so that the data record contains the voting time and the voting result. Answers are given on a 4 scale. The data is used, among other things, by the city administration to make informed, participatory decisions or to query a mood picture. Before being made available as open data, values were removed whose vote implied a multiple press of a button in less than 60 seconds.</p><p>This project was funded by the state of Baden-Württemberg as part of the funding project zukunftskommune@bw.<a href="https://zukunftsstadt-ulm.de/eselsberg/mobilitaets-station">More information on the project Prototyp Mobilitätsstation am Ese
