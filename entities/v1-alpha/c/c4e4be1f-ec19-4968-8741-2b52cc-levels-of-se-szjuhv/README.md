# Levels of service quality in public transport

> Service quality levels are an indicator of public transit service. Depending on the rate of service of a public transport stop and the type of transport serving it, the area adjacent to it is assigned a level of service quality. The further an area is from the stop, the lower its level of service. The levels of quality of service thus make it possible to define places which by the high level of public transport service have a high potential for development. This data is available as an indication and does not, in itself, have legal values. The quality levels of public transport service are calculated for each stop in accordance with the criteria of the cantonal master plan (https://geo.fr.ch/pdcantc/pdcantsvc.svc/pdf/theme/t201/fr/current) It should be noted that in the context of assigning a quality level to the service to the building area, a more detailed analysis of the accessibility of the stops by pedestrians must be carried out. Service quality levels are updated annually, following the publication of the new public transport schedule for the current year. The quality of service may therefore change if the location and/or rate of certain stops have been changed.
