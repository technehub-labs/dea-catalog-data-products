# Partial guideline Footpaths and hiking trails

> The cantonal footpath and hiking trail network is divided into four different networks – a footpath network of the canton of Basel-Stadt, a footpath network of the city of Basel, a hiking trail network of the canton of Basel-Stadt and an informative plan content, which represent the footpath network of the municipalities of Riehen and Bettingen. The networks are divided into existing and planned footpaths and hiking trails of their respective level groups.
