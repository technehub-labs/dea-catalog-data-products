# ADEM key figures

> The detailed data underlying the monthly ADEM key figures. This dataset provides information on jobseekers registered with the Employment Development Agency (ADEM) and on vacancies that employers report to ADEM. - Detailed profiles of resident jobseekers available - Openings and closures of jobseekers' files - Beneficiaries of employment measures - Non-resident job seekers - Jobseekers compensated - Long series on positions reported each month and vacancies at the end of each month, from June 2006. - Detailed series of posts declared each month and vacancies at the end of each month, from January 2014. - Resident jobseekers available by detailed age group - Jobseekers by occupation ([ROME](http://rome.adem.public.lu/index _base.html)) - Resident jobseekers available by nationality - The place of residence of available resident jobseekers - Available resident jobseekers under 30 years of age
