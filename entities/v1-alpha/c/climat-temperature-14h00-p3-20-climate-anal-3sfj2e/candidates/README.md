# Climate analysis 2020 - Daytime air temperature 2070-2099

> The data show the air temperature (in °C) in the canton of Geneva that was modeled with the urban climate model FITNAH-3D. The results are based on a horizontal spatial resolution of 10 m and on a native summer weather situation (weather condition of low wind radiation). They are valid for the area of human residence 2 m from the ground and represent the daytime situation at 14:00 hours (maximum thermal load). The climate parameter is considered both in its current and future characteristic (including climate change and future development). The assumed temperature increases for future periods refer to the results of the climate scenarios for Switzerland (CH2018). The temperature rises correspond to the mean summer change signal of scenario RCP 8.5 for Western Switzerland. The product consists of one raster per climatic period. Climate period: P3: Future period 2070-2099 ("2085") -> + 4.6 °C compared to P0 Development scenario: P1, P2 and P3: Land cover with projects expected to be completed by 2035 For areas outside the canton, baseline soil cover data are less accurate in Geneva. Insulated trees and waterproofing surfaces around buildings, two factors that influence climatic parameters, could not be integrated into the analysis model. Therefore, out-of-canton data should be treated with caution. Reminder: In accordance with the GCU, information relating to climate analysis has no legal value.
