# expedition.nationalpark.2022 - Mallnitz: Agriculture in Transition

> expedition.nationalpark.2022 took place from 29 to 31 May 2022 in the national park community of Mallnitz. The three-day trial took place as part of the long-term cooperation between the National Park Hohe Tauern Carinthia and the University of Applied Sciences Carinthia. The theme of the expedition was devoted to the change in agriculture and the associated changes in various areas of daily life, the landscape, regional development and the protected area. These are visible and discussed during visits to 10 local farms directly or indirectly related to agriculture. The expedition team consisted of three scientists from Carinthia University of Applied Sciences and three local actors from the national park region and was accompanied by a national park ranger and a research assistant. By combining local knowledge and scientific impulses, new perspectives on the national park have been developed.
