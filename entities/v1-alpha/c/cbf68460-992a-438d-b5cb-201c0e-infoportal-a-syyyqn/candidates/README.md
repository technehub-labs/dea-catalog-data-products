# Infoportal Accessibility - Health - Entrances

> In the Infoportal Accessibility, facilities with public use are: - barrier-free accessibility and accessibility - the equipment with barrier-free toilets and their characteristics - the provision of technical aids for persons with disabilities; - special offers and services for people with disabilities. For each recorded facility there is detailed information, each of which is summarized on its own website. All information is available in three languages (DE, CS and EN). The information portal has its own website at www.dresden.de. There is an advanced search function for users. In addition, data collection is possible for everyone after a one-time registration (button <Join> in the information portal). There is a video guide explaining how to record data. As a rule, the entities covered by other users themselves are only published after an on-site inspection. Most of the facilities included were taken up by a service provider within the framework of the INTERREG-funded project "The Bohemian Saxon Border Area barrier-free". The project also financed the software development, which was implemented by the company IT of the state capital Dresden.
