# Statistics on museums

> The National Board of Antiquities collects and publishes annual statistics on the field of museums. Statistics are collected from professionally managed museums in Finland. The annual tables contain mainly numerical data produced in the field of museum statistics. In some cases, textual information is also included. In these cases, the consistency of the material may not have been guaranteed. The data can be downloaded in csv and excel formats. You can download the product information sheet in PDF format from the section More information.
