# Basic data — Technical Charter — Road, Mobility and Transport

> The stratum collects information on mobility and transport of whatever nature it may be. It describes the modeling for areas that mainly collects the constructive geometric characteristics of transport infrastructure. The following contents are therefore grouped in this section of the specification: — Roads and their particulars, including both the infrastructure constituting the primary and secondary roads, within the theme “roads” include areas specifically intended for traffic and/or pedestrian traffic and/or for cycles. — Rail transport infrastructure and its particulars, including railways, metros, tramways and funiculars. — Other transport facilities and related particulars, including cableway transport (works, ski lifts, etc.) under this heading.
