# Conference on the Future of Europe

> The Conference on the Future of Europe is an opportunity for European citizens to debate on Europe’s challenges and priorities. The European Parliament, the Council and the European Commission have committed to listen to Europeans and to follow up on the recommendations made. The main hub for the Conference is the multilingual digital platform. It is the place for citizens to share ideas and send online submissions. They will be collected, analysed, monitored and published throughout the Conference on the future of Europe. The dataset contains the raw data from the multilingual digital platform.
