# EEE Marketed UK Summary

> This record is for Approval for Access product AfA316. EEE Marketed UK Summary contains data reported by Producer Compliance Schemes (PCSs) on the amount of EEE(Electrical and Electronic Equipment) their producer members are placing on the market. The data is broken down by: Category (1 –13) and Houshold/non-household. The report is a UK dataset and contains no information about any specific company. Data is reported quarterly, Reports available date back to Q3 2007. Attribution statement: © Environment Agency copyright and/or database right 2019. All rights reserved.
