# Education and Training in England: Offenders' Learning and Skills Service

> Offenders' Learning and Skills Service education and training data. The data show participation and achievement by equality and diversity, and level as well as enrolments and achievements at aim level by sector subject area. The Offenders' Learning and Skills Service provide access, according to need, to learning and skills for offenders in prisons and supervised in the community to enable them to gain the skills and qualifications they need to hold down a job and have a positive role in society.
