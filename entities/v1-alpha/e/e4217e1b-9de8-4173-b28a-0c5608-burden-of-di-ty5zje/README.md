# Burden of disease of air pollution (Countries, NUTS regions and cities), tabular data (2005 - on-going)

> This dataset presents the burden of disease from air pollution due to exposure to three main pollutants (PM2.5, NO2 and O3) at country, different NUTS and city levels. The calculations are made from year 2005 and are updated every year (YY) adding the results for year YY-2. The concentrations data are taken from the ETC/HE interpolated maps (EEA geospatial data catalogue: https://sdi.eea.europa.eu/catalogue/srv/eng/catalog.search#/metadata/82700fbd-2953-467b-be0a-78a520c3a7ef). The methodology is as described in several ETC and EEA Reports, available at "ETC HE Reports on the environmental burden of disease attributable to air pollution in Europe - Eionet Portal" (https://www.eionet.europa.eu/etcs/etc-he/products/etc-he-reports-on-the-environmental-burden-of-disease-attributable-to-air-pollution-in-europe). The concentrations maps are available at the EEA's Datahub: https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/datahub/datahubitem-view/82700fbd-2953-467b-be0a-78a520c3a7ef
