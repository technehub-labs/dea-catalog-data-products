# LEP Environment 2004 Priority Areas for Nature Conservation Saarland

> Extensive presentation of the priority areas for nature conservation within the framework of the State Development Plan for the Environment. Priority areas for nature conservation (UN) serve to safeguard the supra-local interests of nature conservation and landscape conservation. They are based on the nature reserves designated and planned by regulation and the identification of areas of federal and national importance in accordance with the species and biotope protection programme in Saarland as well as the areas notified and subsequently notified to the European Natura 2000 network of protected areas.
