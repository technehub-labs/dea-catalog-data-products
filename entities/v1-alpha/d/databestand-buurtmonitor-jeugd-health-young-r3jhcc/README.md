# Health young people Flevoland: You and your health research (tm school year 2018/2019)

> Data on the health of young people at neighbourhood- , district- and municipality level in Flevoland based on the annual preventive health surveys among secondary school students of grades 1 and 3 of VMBO and grades 2 and 4 of Havo VWO (project You and your health). The data is collected during the school year and updated once a year. Area: Almere, Dronten, Lelystad Noordoostpolder, Urk and Zeewolde.
