# Statistical data of the natural gas sector (ILR)

> This dataset documents developments in the natural gas market (distribution and supply) in Luxembourg. It is mainly aimed at national and international statistical and economic bodies, as well as professionals in the sector and consumers. The data represent a comprehensive resource for the analysis of different aspects of the Luxembourg natural gas market. The Institute collects data from network operators and gas suppliers and compiles statistics as part of its monitoring mission. The dataset covers various topics such as infrastructure, consumption, production, prices, customer data, imports and the wholesale market. The information is also accessible via an interactive table proposed by the Institute.
