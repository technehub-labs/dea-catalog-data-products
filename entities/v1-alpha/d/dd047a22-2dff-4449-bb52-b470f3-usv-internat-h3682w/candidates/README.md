# USV International Mobility INCOMING ay 2023-2024

> This dataset contains detailed information on incoming Erasmus+ students hosted by Stefan cel Mare University from Suceava during the academic year 2023–2024. It includes data on students' home universities and countries, gender, study cycle, mobility period, and associated departments within USV. Short-term mobilities carried out under Blended Intensive Programmes (BIP), with a duration of 4–5 days, have been excluded from this dataset. This decision was made to ensure analytical consistency and to more accurately reflect international mobilities with significant institutional impact.
