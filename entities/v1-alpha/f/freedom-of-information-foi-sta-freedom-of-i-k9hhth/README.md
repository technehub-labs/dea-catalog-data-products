# Freedom of Information (FOI) Statistics - Environment Agency

> Annual statistics of the Environment Agency's Freedom of Information (FOI) requests. This dataset will be updated annually with data from 2021/2022; 2022/2023; 2023/2024. Yearly (1st April - 31st March) Data shows: Total requests received Total requests closed Total requests closed within statutory timescale Total requests closed outside statutory timescale Total closed requests where information was granted in full Total closed requests where information was withheld in full Total closed requests where information was partially provided Total internal reviews received Attribution statement: © Environment Agency copyright and/or database right 2024.
