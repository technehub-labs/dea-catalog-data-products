# Climate Analysis Map Tag PET

> The Tag PET climate analysis map shows the physiologically equivalent temperature (PET). As a heat index, PET combines the effects of air temperature, humidity, wind speed and solar radiation on the energy balance of the human body. It can be understood as a perceived temperature (unit: °C).
