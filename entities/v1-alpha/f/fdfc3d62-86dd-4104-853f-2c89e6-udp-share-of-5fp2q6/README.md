# UDP - Share of trips by train

> This indicator measures the relative importance of sustainable means of transportation within a tourism destination, proxied by the share of trips using train as mode of transportation. Higher values indicate a more widespread use of train for domestic travel compared to vehicles with a higher environmental impact. The indicator is part of the environmental impacts pillar of the EU Tourism Dashboard. UNIT OF MEASURE: %. RESOLUTION: NUTS0. COMPLETENESS: Missing countries: CY; LU; MT; SI. METHODOLOGY: The indicator is obtained dividing the number of trips by train by the number of trips done using all means of transport, i.e.: Air, land, railways, buses, coahes, motor vehicles, waterway, other. DATA SOURCES: Number of trips by mode of transport (tour_dem_tttr) [ESTAT].
