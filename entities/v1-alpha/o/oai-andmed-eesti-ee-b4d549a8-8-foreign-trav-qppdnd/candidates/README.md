# Foreign travel statistics

> Foreign travel statistics give an overview of the movement of natural persons travelling abroad across the Estonian border. A foreign trip is a trip of a resident of one country to another country, the trip lasts no longer than one year and the main purpose of the trip is not a paid activity or long-term studies in the country of destination. The data have been found using statistical models with input from the anonymous mobile positioning method data on the movement of passengers across the Estonian border.
