# OE0454: Life expectancy by sex, age group and education

> The dataset shows life expectancy at birth and years left to live by sex and age group (including 0, 1-4, 5-9, 10-14, 15-19, 20-24, 25-29, 30-34 to 85 and older) and education (primary or basic education, secondary education, higher education). The purpose of collecting life expectancy data is to provide a standard population health indicator for monitoring trends over time, assessing differences between population groups and regions, and informing health policy and prevention planning. Data source: Statistics Estonia
