# TTO60: E-health interfacing and reporting by legal form, type of health care provider and owner

> The dataset contains an overview of independent health care providers in Estonia that are interfaced with the national e-health system and have submitted data there. Data are presented by legal form, type of health care provider, and type of owner in order to describe the extent of interfacing and reporting across provider groups. The dataset enables assessment of which provider groups are connected to e-health and have submitted data in order to support monitoring of Health Information System coverage and data flows. Data source: Calculations are based on Estonian National Health Information System; the indicators are calculated and published by the National Institute for Health Development
