# TE753: Disability free life expectancy by sex, age and county

> The dataset shows the number of years of healthy life expectancy by county (15 counties), age (0, 65-69), sex and year. The purpose of collecting these data is to provide a standard population health indicator for monitoring trends over time and comparing population groups and regions. Data source: Statistics Estonia
