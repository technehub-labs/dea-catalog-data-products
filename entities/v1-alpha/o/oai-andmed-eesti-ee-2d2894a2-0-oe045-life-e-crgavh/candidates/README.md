# OE045: Life expectancy by sex and age

> The dataset shows life expectancy at birth and the number of years left to live by sex and age (0-100) in a given year. The purpose of collecting life expectancy data is to provide a standard population health indicator for monitoring trends over time, assessing differences between population groups and regions, and informing health policy and prevention planning. Data source: Statistics Estonia
