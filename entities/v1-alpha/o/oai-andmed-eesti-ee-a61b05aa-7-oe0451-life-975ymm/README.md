# OE0451: Life expectancy by sex, age group and type of settlement

> The dataset shows life expectancy at birth and years left to live by sex and age group (including 0, 1-4, 5-9, 10-14, 15-19, 20-24, 25-29, 30-34 to 85 and older) and type of settlement unit or area (city and town settlement region, rural settlement region, urban settlements, rural settlements). The purpose of collecting life expectancy data is to provide a standard population health indicator for monitoring trends over time, assessing differences between population groups and regions, and informing health policy and prevention planning. Data source: Statistics Estonia
