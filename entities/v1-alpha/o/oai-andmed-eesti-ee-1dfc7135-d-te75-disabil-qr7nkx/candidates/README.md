# TE75: Disability free life expectancy by sex and age group

> The dataset shows the number of years of healthy life expectancy by age group (including 0, 1-4, 5-9, 10-14, 15-19, 20-24, 25-29, 30-34, 35-39 to 85 and older), sex and year. The purpose of collecting these data is to provide a standard population health indicator for monitoring trends over time and comparing population groups and regions. Data source: Statistics Estonia
