# OE044: Life expectancy by sex and age (1959-1988)

> The dataset presents the average life expectancy for men and women at birth, at age 25, and at age 50 in a given year. The purpose of collecting life expectancy data is to provide a standard population health indicator for monitoring trends over time, assessing differences between population groups and regions, and informing health policy and prevention planning. Data source: Statistics Estonia
