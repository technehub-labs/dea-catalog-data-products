# TE754: Disability free life expectancy by sex, age and educational level

> The dataset shows the number of disability free life expectancy by education (first level education or lower, second level education, third level education), age (0, 65-69), sex and year. The purpose of collecting these data is to provide a standard population health indicator for monitoring trends over time and comparing population groups and regions. Data source: Statistics Estonia
