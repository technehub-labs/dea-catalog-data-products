# Transport and communication routes

> This dataset contains the axes of the communication and transport routes of the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg. The data comes from the theme "communication routes" of the BD-L-TC (2015), enriched and supplemented by the official data (names of localities and streets) from the National Register of Localities and Streets (CACLR) and the BD-Adresse. The update is carried out continuously by ACT taking into account different sources, including: - information received from the municipal authorities, which are responsible for the names of streets and the definition of addresses - BD-L-ORTHO (orthophotographs) - BD-MO (official measurement database) - the completeness of the data cannot be guaranteed The link to the national register of localities and streets is guaranteed by the integration of identifiers as external keys.
