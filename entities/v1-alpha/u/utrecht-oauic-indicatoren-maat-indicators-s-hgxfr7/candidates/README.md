# Indicators Social participation (Social environment)

> #### Description Utrecht in Figures is the online database of the municipality of Utrecht. Here, the Research & Advice department publishes figures about the city of Utrecht and the Utrecht districts and neighbourhoods. With Utrecht in Figures, we want to show all those interested in Utrecht how things are actually going with Utrecht. Utrecht in Figures is divided into themes. Within the theme 'Social participation (Social living environment)' you will find information about the following 4 'indicators': - % of residents involved in one or more activities nearby % of the population participating in society % of residents with disabilities participating - % satisfaction availability of spaces for nearby residents activities: (very) satisfied #### Number of indicators Number of indicators under this theme: 4 #### Source Research & Advice, Municipality of Utrecht, Population survey #### Related datasets Other datasets that fall under this theme: - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-inclusion-and-social-safety-social-living environment-">Indicators Inclusion and social security (Social living environment)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-social-well-being-social-living-environment-">Indicators Social well-being (Social living environment)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-social-contacts-social-living environment-">Indicators Social contacts (Social living environment)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-social-coherence-social-living environment-">Indicators Social cohesion (Social living environment)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-home-and-school-social-living environment-">Indicators Home and school (Social living environment)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-volunteer work-social-living environment-">Indicators Volunteer work (Social living environment)</a> #### All Utrecht In Figures datasets
