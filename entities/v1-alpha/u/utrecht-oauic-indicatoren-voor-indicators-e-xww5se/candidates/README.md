# Indicators Early childhood education and care (Education & skills)

> #### Description Utrecht in Figures is the online database of the municipality of Utrecht. Here, the Research & Advice department publishes figures about the city of Utrecht and the Utrecht districts and neighbourhoods. With Utrecht in Figures, we want to show all those interested in Utrecht how things are actually going with Utrecht. Utrecht in Figures is divided into themes. Within the theme 'Preschool education and childcare (Education & skills)' you will find information about the following 3 'indicators': % share of toddlers with pre-school education advice attending pre-school -% of parents who requested parenting advice from childcare/preschool/school in the past year -% toddlers between 2.5 and 4 years old with early childhood education advice #### Number of indicators Number of indicators under this theme: 3 #### Source Public health, municipality of Utrecht; Research & Advice, Municipality of Utrecht, Population survey #### Related datasets Other datasets that fall under this theme: - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-vocational-and-scientific-education-education-skills-">Indicators Vocational and scientific education (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic indicators-educational skills">Indicators Education & skills</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-primary-education-education-skills-">Indicators Primary education (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-skills-education-skills-">Indicators Skills (Education & Skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-continuing-education-education-skills-">Indicators Secondary education (Education & skills)</a> #### All Utrecht In Figures datasets View <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/search/query/-/filters/tag%3Autrecht%20in%20digits%3Btype%3Adataset/page/1">here all Utrecht In Figures datasets on this website</a>.
