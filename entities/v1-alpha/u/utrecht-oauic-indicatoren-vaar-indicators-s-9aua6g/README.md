# Indicators Skills (Education & Skills)

> #### Description Utrecht in Figures is the online database of the municipality of Utrecht. Here, the Research & Advice department publishes figures about the city of Utrecht and the Utrecht districts and neighbourhoods. With Utrecht in Figures, we want to show all those interested in Utrecht how things are actually going with Utrecht. Utrecht in Figures is divided into themes. Within the theme 'Skills (Education & Skills)' you will find information about the following 6 'indicators': - % 1- and 2-year-olds with slow development -% have a swimming diploma, children 10-12 years - % difficulty in understanding health information, elderly 65+ -% difficulty in understanding health information, adults 18+ - % difficulty filling in medical forms, elderly 65+ -% difficulty filling out medical forms, adults 18+ years #### Number of indicators Number of indicators under this theme: 6 #### Source Health Monitor Adults & Elderly; Youth monitor Utrecht; Youth health care Utrecht #### Related datasets Other datasets that fall under this theme: - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-vocational-and-scientific-education-education-skills-">Indicators Vocational and scientific education (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic indicators-educational skills">Indicators Education & skills</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-primary-education-education-skills-">Indicators Primary education (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic indicators-pre-school education-and-childcare-education-skills-">Indicators Pre-school education and childcare (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-continuing-education-education-skills-">Indicators Secondary education (Education & skills)</a> #### All Utrecht In Figures datasets View <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/search/query/-/filters/tag%3Autrecht%20in%20digits%3Btype%3Adataset/page/1">here all U
