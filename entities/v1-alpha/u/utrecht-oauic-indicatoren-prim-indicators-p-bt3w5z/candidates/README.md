# Indicators Primary education (Education & skills)

> #### Description Utrecht in Figures is the online database of the municipality of Utrecht. Here, the Research & Advice department publishes figures about the city of Utrecht and the Utrecht districts and neighbourhoods. With Utrecht in Figures, we want to show all those interested in Utrecht how things are actually going with Utrecht. Utrecht in Figures is divided into themes. Within the theme 'Primary education (Education & skills)' you will find information about the following 6 'indicators': -% like to go to school, children 10-12 years - % reading proficiency reference levels -% Calculate reference levels -% language proficiency reference levels - number of primary schools by denomination - number of pupils by type of education #### Number of indicators Number of indicators under this theme: 6 #### Source Education Implementation Service; Youth monitor Utrecht #### Related datasets Other datasets that fall under this theme: - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-vocational-and-scientific-education-education-skills-">Indicators Vocational and scientific education (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic indicators-educational skills">Indicators Education & skills</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-skills-education-skills-">Indicators Skills (Education & Skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic indicators-pre-school education-and-childcare-education-skills-">Indicators Pre-school education and childcare (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-continuing-education-education-skills-">Indicators Secondary education (Education & skills)</a> #### All Utrecht In Figures datasets View <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/search/query/-/filters/tag%3Autrecht%20in%20digits%3Btype%3Adataset/page/1">here all Utrecht In Figures datasets on this website</a>.
