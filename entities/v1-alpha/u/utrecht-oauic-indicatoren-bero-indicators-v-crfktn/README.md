# Indicators Vocational and scientific education (Education & skills)

> #### Description Utrecht in Figures is the online database of the municipality of Utrecht. Here, the Research & Advice department publishes figures about the city of Utrecht and the Utrecht districts and neighbourhoods. With Utrecht in Figures, we want to show all those interested in Utrecht how things are actually going with Utrecht. Utrecht in Figures is divided into themes. Within the theme 'Professional and scientific education (Education & skills)' you will find information about the following 8 'indicators': -% of young people aged 17 to 22 with a starting qualification or school enrolment -% early school leavers - number of HBO and WO graduates by sector - number of enrolled university of applied sciences and university students by sector - number of mbo, hbo and wo students - number of Utrecht MBO graduates by sector - number of Utrecht MBO students by sector - report figure Utrecht MBO settings #### Number of indicators Number of indicators under this theme: 8 #### Source CBS; Municipality of Utrecht, Education Department; JOB; Education Implementation Service #### Related datasets Other datasets that fall under this theme: - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic indicators-educational skills">Indicators Education & skills</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-primary-education-education-skills-">Indicators Primary education (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-skills-education-skills-">Indicators Skills (Education & Skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic indicators-pre-school education-and-childcare-education-skills-">Indicators Pre-school education and childcare (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-continuing-education-education-skills-">Indicators Secondary education (Education & skills)</a> #### All Utrecht In Figures datasets View <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/search/query/-/filters/tag%3Autrecht%20
