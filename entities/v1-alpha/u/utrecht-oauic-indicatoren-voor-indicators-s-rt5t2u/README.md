# Indicators Secondary education (Education & skills)

> #### Description Utrecht in Figures is the online database of the municipality of Utrecht. Here, the Research & Advice department publishes figures about the city of Utrecht and the Utrecht districts and neighbourhoods. With Utrecht in Figures, we want to show all those interested in Utrecht how things are actually going with Utrecht. Utrecht in Figures is divided into themes. Under the theme 'Further education (Education & skills)' you will find information about the following 8 'indicators': - % of exam candidates by type of education -% graduates by type of education % of pupils in Utrecht schools living in Utrecht -% of Utrecht students attending school in Utrecht -% like it at school, young people class 2 and 4 - number of pupils by residential location and district of educational institution Number of schools by denomination - average final grades for Dutch and mathematics by type of education #### Number of indicators Number of indicators under this theme: 8 #### Source Youth Health Monitor; Education Implementation Service #### Related datasets Other datasets that fall under this theme: - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-vocational-and-scientific-education-education-skills-">Indicators Vocational and scientific education (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic indicators-educational skills">Indicators Education & skills</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-primary-education-education-skills-">Indicators Primary education (Education & skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic-indicators-skills-education-skills-">Indicators Skills (Education & Skills)</a> - <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/dataset/oauic indicators-pre-school education-and-childcare-education-skills-">Indicators Pre-school education and childcare (Education & skills)</a> #### All Utrecht In Figures datasets View <a href="https://open.utrecht.nl/search/query/-/filters/tag%3Autrecht%20in%20dig
