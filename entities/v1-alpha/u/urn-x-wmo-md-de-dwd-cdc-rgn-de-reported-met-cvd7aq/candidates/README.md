# Reported methane (CH4) emissions from the agriculture sector in kt/year

> The size and spatial variability of reported methane emissions from the agriculture sector are presented for the federal states and the rural districts of Germany. The figure given is the sum of all emissions in the agriculture sector for the whole year. Agriculture is defined as the sum of the GNFR (Gridded Nomenclature For Reporting) sectors (Agricultural _Livestock, Agricultural _Other, Off _Road). Future improvements can be expected through future developments and refinements of the methods.
