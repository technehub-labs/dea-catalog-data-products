# National Council elections 2023 - Panaschierstatistics Canton of Thurgau by municipalities

> The dataset contains the results of the candidates for the 2023 National Council elections in the canton of Thurgau, including the Panaschi votes at cantonal and political municipal level. The data show how many votes candidates received from unchanged and modified ballots (Panaschier votes = votes from other lists).
