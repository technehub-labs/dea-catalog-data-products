# Regional offices Environment CyL

> Cartographic of the regional offices (OCAS) of the Autonomous Community of Castile and Leon. The regional environmental offices are the administrative units located in the territory of each of the forest districts of Castilla y León. They perform functions of public information and management of procedures within the scope of their competences. The mapping of the district offices is developed as a support tool to facilitate the location of them.
