# Skills for Life in England

> Annually updated data for Skills for Life Qualifications in England. The data shows participation and achievement figures broken down by Age, Level, Provider Type, Geography and Learner Demographics. Skills for Life qualifications are designed to give people the reading, writing, maths and communication skills they need in everyday life, to operate effectively in work and to help them succeed on other training courses.
