# WMS Health Control

> This service contains the location of cooling towers and evaporative condensers that are installations that use water and produce aerosols during their operation and, therefore, susceptible to become a focus of proliferation and dissemination of Legionella bacteria, which makes them subject to compliance with Royal Decree 487/2022 of 21 June: https://www.boe.es/search/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-10297 , laying down health requirements for the prevention and control of legionellosis. The owners of these facilities, prior to the start of operation of the same, have the duty to notify them to the Directorate General of Public Health of the Government of the Canary Islands, which publishes this layer with the cooling towers and evaporative capacitors notified and in operation at 31/12/2023, the update being planned annually.
