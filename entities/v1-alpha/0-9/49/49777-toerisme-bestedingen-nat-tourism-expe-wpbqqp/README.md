# Tourism; expenditure, national accounts

> This table contains figures on the tourist expenditure of residents and non-residents in Dutch companies, but also figures on what residents spend abroad. Tourism is not an industry in itself, but covers a large number of products and services provided to tourists from different industries. The figures are consistent with the conceptual framework of the National Accounts (NA) and can be compared with pre-existing macroeconomic indicators such as total value added in basic prices and employment in the Netherlands. The tourism accounts thus provide an integrated macroeconomic overview of the significance of tourism for the economy. Data available from 2010. Status of figures: Data from 2010 to 2024 are final. 2025 data are provisional. Changes as of 25 September 2026: In the previous version, the provisional figures for 2023 were erroneously published instead of final figures. This version includes the correct final figures for 2023. Furthermore, 2025 dates have been added to this table. When will there be new figures? Preliminary figures shall be published 8 months after the end of the reporting year. After 20 months, the final figures will be published.
