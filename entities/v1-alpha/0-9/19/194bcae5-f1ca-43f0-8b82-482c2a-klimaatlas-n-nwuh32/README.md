# Klimaatlas NRW - Planning and Construction: Heating degree days

> Service consisting of the grid layers for the average number of heating degree days [Kelvin*Tag]i. Here the heating energy requirement is represented by a temperature sum. The heating degree days are derived from the air temperature and represent the temperature sum from the difference between the average room temperature of 20 °C and the daily average of the outside temperature, taking into account only days with an outside temperature below 15 °C. The respective grid layers were calculated for the 30-year averages of the climate normal periods 1951-1980, 1961-1990, 1971-2000, 1981-2010 and 1991-2020 for the observed past. In addition, the changes of the climate normal period 1991-2020 relating to 1961-1990 and 1951-1980 are presented. In addition, climate projections for the future periods 2031-2060 and 2071-2100 are available, each structured according to the climate projections RCP2.6, RCP4.5 and RCP8.5. The strength of the possible climate signal per scenario is divided according to the 15th, 50th and 85th. percentile. Both absolute mean values and so-called delta-change grids are shown, which show the change of the climate signal compared to the reference period 1971-2000. Data source: German Weather Service (DWD); Sources of climate projection data: Brienen et al. (2020), Krähenmann (2019), Calculation by the LANUK. Further information from the German Weather Service must be observed: https://www.dwd.de/DE/service/rechtliche_hinweise/rechtliche_hinweise_node.html
