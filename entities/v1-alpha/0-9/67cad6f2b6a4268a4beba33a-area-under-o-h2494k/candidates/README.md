# Area under organic farming (certified organic or in-conversion)

> The indicator measures areas under organic farming (certified organic and in-conversion). Areas "certified organic" which bring together parcels whose conversion period has ended and whose production can be marketed with the designation "organic farming" , areas in conversion (the conversion period varies from 2 years for annual crops to 3 years for perennial crops).
