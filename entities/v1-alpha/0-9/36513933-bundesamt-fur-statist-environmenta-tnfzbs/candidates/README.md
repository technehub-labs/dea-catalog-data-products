# Environmentally related taxes account by tax category and economic player

> This dataset presents annual data on revenue from environmentally related taxes of the economy and the households by type of tax (energy taxes, transport taxes, pollution taxes), economic sector and division and the type of use of household (transport, heating), since 1990.
