# Road transport; transported weight by distance class

> This table provides data on the cargo weight carried by Dutch commercial vehicles with a maximum permissible weight (loading capacity plus unladen weight) of more than 3.5 tonnes. The figures shall be broken down by distance over which the goods have been transported. In addition, a distinction is made between professional and private transport. Data available from: Annual figures available from 2005. Status of figures: The figures up to 2022 are final, the 2023 and 2024 figures are further provisional and the 2025 figures have a provisional status. Changes as of 29 June 2026: The provisional figures for the reporting year 2025 have been added and the figures for 2024 have been updated. When will there be new figures? New figures will become available 5 months after the end of the reporting year.
