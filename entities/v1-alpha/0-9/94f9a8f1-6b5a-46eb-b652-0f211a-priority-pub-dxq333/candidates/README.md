# Priority public transport network

> The priority network takes into account routes with a particularly dense public transport offer and high passenger demand. It covers all routes on which, on a working day, more than six journeys per hour and direction are planned for at least one hour, or six journeys per hour and direction for at least twelve hours. On the lines of the priority public transport network, the interests of surface public transport shall be taken into account with particular priority. In addition, the routes served by night traffic between 10 p.m. and 6 p.m. are shown. In addition, the routes that will be added to the priority network or night network in the future are shown with the year of the planned implementation. Furthermore, new construction lines in the process of being implemented are shown.
