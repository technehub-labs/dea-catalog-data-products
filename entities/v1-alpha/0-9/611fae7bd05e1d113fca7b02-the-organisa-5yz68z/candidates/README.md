# The organisation of the public transport lines of the Val d’Essonne

> The creation, organisation and management of public transport lines is the responsibility of Île-de-France Mobilités (IDFM) in Ile-de-France. Nevertheless, in order to preserve the quality of the service provided to users, the Community of Communes du Val d’Essonne participates in the financing of many regular bus and school transport routes (Circuits Special Schools or Regular School-related Lines) that marries the territory.
