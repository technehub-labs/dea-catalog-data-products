# Metropolitan Plan for Adaptation to Climate Change

> The Metropolitan Plan for Adaptation to Climate Change (PMAAC) aims to integrate adaptation to climate change in inter-municipal and municipal planning, and to create a culture of cooperation across the various sectors and socio-economic actors of the region, capable of strengthening territorial resilience throughout the territory. The metropolitan vision that underpinned the plan's construction is the reduction of AML's vulnerability to climate change. The region as a whole should promote adaptation as a tool to build a more sustainable territory, capable of ensuring a more equitable and territorially fairer development model that affirms its capacity for innovation and its global attractiveness.
