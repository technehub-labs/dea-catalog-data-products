# Digital technologies in agriculture.

> Digitisation in agriculture is often associated with reduced input costs and more environmentally friendly agricultural production. However, an assessment of the actual environmental compatibility and the environmental performance of the use of technology is often lacking. This study presents a concept and criteria for assessing the environmental impact of the use of digital technologies in agriculture. The developed environmental assessment concept describes possibilities for qualitative and quantitative evaluation of environmental effects using indicators. It is generally valid, can also be applied to future technology developments and also takes into account indirect effects.
