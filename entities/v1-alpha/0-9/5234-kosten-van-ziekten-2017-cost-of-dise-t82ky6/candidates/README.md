# Cost of diseases 2017

> The figures in this table come from the Cost of Diseases study 2015 (KVZ) of the National Institute for Public Health and the Environment (RIVM), carried out in collaboration with the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS). This table was created for the RIVM website Gezondheidenzorg.info and contains data on healthcare expenditure in the Netherlands broken down by dimensions of care use (diagnosis, age, gender) and care provision (sector, care function and form of financing) for the year 2017. The Cost of Diseases figures are available in three separate tables. Due to the size, the figures for 2015 and 2017 are published in two separate tables. The third table shows the trend data for the years 2003, 2005, 2007, 2011, 2013, 2015 and 2017. Due to major changes in the diagnosis format and in the presentation of financing and care function, only the ICD10 main groups are included in this trend table in the diagnosis dimension and the dimensions of financing form and care function are missing. Data available from: 2017 Status of figures: Definitive. Changes as of 29-10-2019: None, this is a new table. When will there be new figures? Every two years. The Cost of Diseases study itself comes out every four years, but for each intermediate two-year period, an estimated allocation based on demographic extrapolation takes place. Figures for a reference year shall become available two years after the end of that reference year. The next update will cover the year 2019 and will be published in 2021.
