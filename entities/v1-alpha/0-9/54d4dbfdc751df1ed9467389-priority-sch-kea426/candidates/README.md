# Priority School Education Network

> The objective of the priority education policy is to correct the impact of social and economic inequalities on school success by strengthening pedagogical and educational action in schools and establishments in territories facing the greatest social difficulties. The Orientation and Programming Law for the Refoundation of the School of the Republic defined its objective: reduce the gap in educational achievement between pupils in priority education and other pupils in France to less than 10%. The reform of the priority education policy is under way. The renovated national site for priority education accompanies this refoundation. This file presents schools' priority education networks
