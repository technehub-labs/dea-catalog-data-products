# Solenergipotential i huvudstadsregionen

> The data includes the potential yield of solar power calculated by building. The revenue potential has been calculated on the assumption that the roof of the building is fully covered with solar panels, where applicable. The data includes the potential for solar photovoltaic production calculated by building, if the entire area suitable for solar panels is covered with solar panels. When calculating the potential for annual electricity production, the efficiency of solar panels is assumed to be 15% and they are installed parallel to the roof also on flat roofs. The properties of the buildings include electricity production (MWh/year) and the surface area of the panels in square metres in the map service. In the original downloadable material (e.g. the Helsinki Photovoltaic Potential (SHP) folder containing the file electricity.shp), the electricity output is in field ELEC and its unit is kWh/year. The material was completed in the Decumanus project and produced by the Belgian company Eurosense, which is involved in the project and specialises in spatial information services. **WFS interface address:** [https://kartta.hsy.fi/geoserver/wfs](https://kartta.hsy.fi/geoserver/wfs?request=getCapabilities), level: the \_ Solar Potential of Buildings\_pks **WMS interface address:** [https://kartta.hsy.fi/geoserver/wms](https://kartta.hsy.fi/geoserver/wms?request=getCapabilities), level: buildings\_a solar plain potential\_pks, buildings\_a solar plain potential **The material can be viewed and downloaded from [HSY's open map service](https://kartta.hsy.fi/?zoomLevel=5&coord=25496558_6673000&mapLayers=133+100+solar-skin potential_pks&markers=2|3|ffde00|25495997_6672199|).**
