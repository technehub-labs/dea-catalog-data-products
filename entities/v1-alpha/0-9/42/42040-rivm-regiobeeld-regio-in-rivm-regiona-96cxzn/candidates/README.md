# RIVM Regional Image; region (division 2022), 2015-2040

> The figures in this table are based on various data sources edited by the National Institute for Public Health and the Environment (RIVM). This table was created for the RIVM website Regiobeeld.nl and contains data on care use, care provision, health and lifestyle, population development, social environment and physical environment. This data is available at municipal level and at different regional levels and includes demographic projections up to the year 2040 where possible. Data available from: 2015 Status of figures: Definitive. Changes as of 01-03-2023: Expansion with many new indicators, including on the topics of DBC, mental health care and general practitioner care, labour market care and well-being and serious noise nuisance.
