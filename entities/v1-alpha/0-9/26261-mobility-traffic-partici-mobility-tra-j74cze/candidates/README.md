# Mobility; traffic participation and participation in public transport

> This table contains information regarding the mobility of the residents of the Netherlands aged 6 or older in private households, so excluding residents of institutions and homes. The table contains the travel behaviour broken down by the proportion of traffic participation and the proportion of participation in public transport divided by personal characteristics, population and gender. These are regular trips on Dutch territory, including domestic holiday mobility and series of calls trips on Dutch territory. A methodological break has been identified in the 2024 and 2025 ODiN file. When analyzing the first results of the Onderweg in Nederland 2024 survey, a break was observed compared to previous years. Research has shown that, due to shortened invitation and reminder letters, respondents may have received a different impression of what was asked of them regarding their trips, compared with previous years. As a result, ODiN 2024 and 2025 collected partially different data than ODiN 2023. Using the mobility trend from the mobility survey, the methodological break has been corrected as much as possible. More information can be found in the Research Description of the corrected ODiN 2024 and 2025. To inform StatLine users that the results of ODiN 2024 and 2025 were generated differently from previous years, these results are marked with an asterisk. For the corrected results of ODiN 2024 and 2025, no confidence intervals are available, and therefore no lower or upper bounds are provided. Due to a revision of the ODiN files, the figures by motive for 2018 have been changed as of February 10, 2022, but the total number of motives in 2018 has remained the same. In 2019, the revision sometimes resulted in minor changes in travel time. Data available from: 2018 Status of the figures: The figures from 2018 through 2023 are final. The figures from 2024 onward are provisional. Changes as of October 23, 2026: The figures for 2025 have been added. From the 2024 reporting year
