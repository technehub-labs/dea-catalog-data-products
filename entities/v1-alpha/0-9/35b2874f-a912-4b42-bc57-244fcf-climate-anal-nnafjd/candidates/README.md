# Climate analysis map night cold air flow

> The climate analysis map Night Cold Air Flow shows the flow field in 10 m above ground and the cold air volume flow density. The flow field of the cold air drains represents the streamlines calculated from wind speed and direction. The streamlines indicate in flow direction in which an air packet would follow in the wind field (unit: directional arrows). The cold air volume density indicates the amount of cold air flowing through a 1 m wide strip per second. This determines on the one hand the wind speed and on the other hand the vertical width of the flow (unit: m3).
