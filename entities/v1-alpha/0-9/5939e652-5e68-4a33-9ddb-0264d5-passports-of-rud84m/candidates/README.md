# Passports of budget programs of the Department of Agro-Industrial Development of Ivano-Frankivsk Regional State Administration

> The set contains passports of budget programs of the Department of Agro-Industrial Development of Ivano-Frankivsk Regional State Administration under KPKVKMB 2417110 "Implementation of programs in the field of agriculture", KPKVKMB 2417150 "Implementation of programs in the field of forestry and hunting", KPKVKMB 2411110 "Personnel training by vocational and other educational institutions", KPKVKMB 2418831 "Providing long-term loans to individual housing developers in the countryside", KPKVKMB 2411091 "Personnel training by vocational (vocational and technical) education institutions and other educational institutions at the expense of the local budget". Passports are approved by the order of programs of the department of agro-industrial development of the regional state administration. Each passport corresponds to the resource "Results of the budget program" (indicator). When changes are made to – passports, the data set is updated.
