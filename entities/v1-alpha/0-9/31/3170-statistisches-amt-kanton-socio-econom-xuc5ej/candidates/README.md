# Socio-economic characteristics of economic social assistance in the Canton of Zurich

> Economic Social Assistance (WSH) is a temporary, needs-based benefit for people who are in an emergency situation that they cannot cope with on their own. This dataset shows the number of people in the Canton of Zurich receiving economic social assistance, broken down by socio-economic characteristics of educational attainment and employment situation, as well as the number of cases by case structure. For each feature, the respective distribution is also shown. Only persons between the ages of 16 and 64 are taken into account for the purposes of education and employment. Spatially, all values refer to the entire canton of Zurich. A division by municipalities or districts is not included. The dataset starts in 2010 and is constantly being supplemented by new years. The data come from the social assistance recipient statistics of the Federal Statistical Office (FSO). As of 2025, the FSO has switched over to data collection. Comparability with previous years is therefore limited. Further information on the dataset structure and characteristics used can be found in the variable description.
