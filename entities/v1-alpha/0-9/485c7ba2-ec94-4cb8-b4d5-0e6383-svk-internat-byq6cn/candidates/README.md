# SVK International Mobility INCOMING ay 2024-2025

> This dataset contains all the information needed about the incoming international students who are enrolled in the exchange program at Šiauliai State University of Applied Sciences during the 2024-2025 academic year. The data includes information about student demographics, their academic backgrounds, the type and duration of their mobility (semester studies, internships, and Blended Intensive Programmes), and which departments hosted them at the university.
