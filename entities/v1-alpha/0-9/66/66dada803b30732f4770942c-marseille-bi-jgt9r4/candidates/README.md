# Marseille_Biodiversity_Inventory of butterflies in parks

> The City of Marseille makes available the biodiversity data of its territory including this **Inventory of butterflies in the public parks of the City of Marseille.** The resources in this dataset come from a study by the Population Environment Development Laboratory (LPED, [http://www.lped.fr/](http://www.lped.fr/)) carried out by Marie-Hélène Lizée in 2009. For optimal reuse of these inventory data, they have been enriched with the TaxRef taxonomic repository proposed and maintained by the National Museum of Natural History. Each species is associated with a TaxRef identifier (cd_name) and a species presentation (species URL) from the [National Inventory of Natural Heritage] (https://inpn.mnhn.fr/).
