# Cost of diseases; by characteristics of person, illness and care 2003-2011

> The figures in this table come from the Cost of Diseases study (KVZ) of the National Institute for Public Health and the Environment (RIVM), carried out in collaboration with the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS). This table was created for the Public Health Care.info website of the RIVM and contains data on healthcare costs in the Netherlands broken down by use of care (diagnosis of disease, age, gender) and by supply of care (sector, care function and form of financing). This table has been discontinued. The most recent KVZ figures have been published in three new tables. These contain the results of the KVZ 2015 study. See the links to these tables in paragraph 4.
