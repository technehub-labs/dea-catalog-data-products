# International trade and transit; value, weight, goods, mode of transport

> This table provides insight into the international flows of goods from, to and through the Netherlands. The table shows, for import, export, re-export and transit flows and warehousing, the value and gross weight of the goods transported by goods group, mode of transport, type of cargo (whether or not transported in containers), group of countries of origin or destination and group of countries of loading or unloading. This table is co-financed by the Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management. Data available from: 2007 Status of figures: The figures in this table are provisional. Changes as of 9 February 2026: The provisional figures for 2024 have been added and the figures for 2022 and 2023 have been updated. When will there be new figures? The new provisional figures generally become available one year after the end of the reporting year. The figures on international flows of goods can be adjusted on the basis of the availability of new or updated source material. As a result, the figures are provisional for a longer period of time.
