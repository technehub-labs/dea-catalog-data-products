# Map of establishments labeled “Quality Tourism”

> List and geolocation of Parisian institutions/providers holders of the right of use of the mark “Quality Tourism”. The “Quality Tourism” brand aims to distinguish between tourist establishments meeting demanding commitments. It thus contributes to the support and development of jobs in the sector and a good image of the destination France. Professionals who display the brand are committed to offering: — a personalised welcome; — clear and precise information; — competent and attentive staff; — a comfortable place with impeccable cleanliness; — local tourist information adapted to your request. (Source: Atout France — Tourist Development Agency of the France; 24/06/13)
