# SOS Education Association

> Created in 2001, SOS Education presents itself as ‘an activist association […] working independently to ensure that the school transmits to every child fundamental knowledge and a taste for excellence’. It states that it is "fully transparent about its funding" and "funded exclusively by the generosity of its members". At the end of its audit and within the limits of its investigations and the information available, the Court declares that the association’s expenditure for the financial years 2013 to 2018 was not in line with the objectives pursued by the public call for generosity.
