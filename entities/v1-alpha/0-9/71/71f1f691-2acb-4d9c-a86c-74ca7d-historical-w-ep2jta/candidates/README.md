# Historical weather data

> <p>This data set contains historical weather data of the station of the DWD (station number: 02712) in the Silvanerweg 6 in Konstanz over a longer period of time.</p> <p>On July 25, 2017, an amendment to the German Weather Service Act ("DWD Act") entered into force. The DWD is legally mandated to provide its weather and climate information largely free of charge. Currently, many geodata such as model predictions, radar data, current measurement and observation data as well as a large number of climate data are available on the Open Data Server <a href="https://opendata.dwd.de/"><strong>https://opendata.dwd.de</strong> </a>. The climate data is provided at <strong><a href="https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/">https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC</a></strong>.</p> <p>The freely accessible data may be re-used without restrictions in accordance with the "Ordinance establishing the Terms of Use for the Provision of Federal Geodata (GeoNutzV)" with a source note (<a href="https://gdz.bkg.bund.de">https://gdz.bkg.bund.de</a>).
