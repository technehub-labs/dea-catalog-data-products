# Road transport; weight transported by road hauliers from the European Union

> This table provides data on the cargo weight carried by road hauliers from the European Union as it has existed since 1 February 2020. The table shows the total cargo carried for each EU Member State, both domestic and international. This table is the successor of the table: Road transport; weight transported by road hauliers from the EU;2005-2019 containing data from the European Union including the United Kingdom. For the link to this table, see paragraph 3. Data available from: 2020 Status of figures: The figures up to 2022 are final, the 2023 and 2024 figures are further provisional and the 2025 figures have a provisional status. Changes as of 3 September 2026: The figures for 2025 have been added. When will there be new figures? The new figures usually become available 10 months after the end of the reporting year.
