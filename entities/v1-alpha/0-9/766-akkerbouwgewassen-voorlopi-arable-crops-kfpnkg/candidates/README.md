# Arable crops; Provisional and final harvest estimate 2010-2022

> This table contains figures on the area cultivated and harvested, the yield per hectare and the total yield in a harvest year, per arable crop. Both the results of the provisional harvest estimate and the results of the final harvest estimate are shown in this table, as well as the percentage difference. In order to arrive at the figure for the yield, a provisional harvest estimate is first made. This happens in the months of August to October. After this estimate, the figures of the final harvest estimate are made in the months of December to March of the year following the harvest year. These figures are still provisional until the end of September in the year following the harvest year. Data available from 2010 to 2022.
