# Road transport; transported weight by distance class, 2005-2012

> This table provides data on the weight carried by Dutch commercial vehicles with a maximum permissible weight (loading capacity plus unladen weight) of more than 3.5 tonnes. The figures shall be broken down by distance over which the goods have been transported. In addition, a distinction is made between professional and private transport. Data available from 2005 to 2012. Status of figures: Data up to 2010 are final and figures for 2011 and 2012 are provisional. As this table has been discontinued, the data are no longer made definitive. Changes as of 21 May 2015 None, this table has been discontinued. As of 2013, the sample design of the freight road transport statistics has been substantially modified. In addition, a simplified questionnaire was introduced in 2013. This has caused a trend break in the time series of the transported weight. When will there be new figures? No longer applicable. Is there a successor? This table is followed by Road Transport; transported weight to distance class. See paragraph 3.
