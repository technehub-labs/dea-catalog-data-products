# Road transport; key figures, 1997-2012

> This table provides data on the transported weight, tonne-kilometres, number of journeys and distances travelled by Dutch commercial vehicles with a maximum authorised weight (loading capacity plus unladen weight) of more than 3.5 tonnes. The figures are broken down by transport flows. In addition, a distinction is made between professional transport and own transport. Data available from 1997 to 2012. Status of figures: The data up to 2011 are final and the figures for 2012 are provisional. As this table has been discontinued, the data are no longer made definitive. Changes as of 21 May 2015 None, this table has been discontinued. As of 2013, the sample design of the freight road transport statistics has been substantially modified. In addition, a simplified questionnaire was introduced in 2013. As a result, a trend break has occurred in the time series of the transported weight and transport performance (loading tonne-kilometres). When will there be new figures? No longer applicable. Is there a successor? This table is followed by Road Transport; key figures. See paragraph 3.
