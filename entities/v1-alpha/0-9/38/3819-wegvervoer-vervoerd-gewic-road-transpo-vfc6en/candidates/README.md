# Road transport; transported weight road hauliers from European Union, 2005-2012

> This table provides insight into the transport flows of freight road transport by freight vehicles from the European Union. The table shows both domestic and international transport for each EU Member State. The transport flows are expressed in terms of transported weight. Data available from 2005 to 2012. Status of figures: Data up to 2010 are final and figures for 2011 and 2012 are provisional. As this table has been discontinued, the data are no longer made definitive. Changes as of 21 May 2015 None, this table has been discontinued. As of 2013, the sample design of the freight road transport statistics has been substantially modified. In addition, a simplified questionnaire was introduced in 2013. This has caused a trend break in the time series of the transported weight. When will there be new figures? No longer applicable. Is there a successor? This table is followed by Road Transport; weight transported by road hauliers from the European Union. See paragraph 3.
