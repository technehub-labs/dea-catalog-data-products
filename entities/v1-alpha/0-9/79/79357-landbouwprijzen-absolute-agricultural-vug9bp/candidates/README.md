# Agricultural prices; absolute input prices

> This table provides figures on the average purchase prices of agricultural input products. The products are classified according to the Eurostat agricultural price nomenclature. Agricultural price statistics have been a European regulation since 2025 (IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2023/1579). Since then, CBS has been responsible for the quality of the statistics. These are therefore published on CBS Statline since 2025. Data from previous years can be found on the Eurostat website (<a href='https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/data/database'>Database - Eurostat</a>). This data has not been checked by Statistics Netherlands for statistical quality. Data available from: 2025 Status of figures: The figures shall first be published as provisional and may be revised up to 1.5 years after the end of the reporting year. When will there be new figures? New figures shall be published no later than 6 months after the end of the reporting year. Changes as of 5 June 2026: None, this is a new table
