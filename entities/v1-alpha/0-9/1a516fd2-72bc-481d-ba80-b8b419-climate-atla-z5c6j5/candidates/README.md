# Climate Atlas NRW - Precipitation: Precipitation days

> Service consisting of grids for precipitation maps. This includes the average number of days of precipitation with ≥ 10 mm, 20 mm, 30 mm and 50 mm per day and year. In addition, there are dry days with <1 mm per day and year as well as the number of snow cover days (with a snow cover ≥ 1 cm snow) per year. The grids are absolutely available for the observation period 1951-2020 as 30-year climate normal periods. Relative grids are also available, which represent a change in precipitation characteristics based on a comparison of the climate periods 1991-2020 from 1961-1990. In addition to the observation grids, projection grids (separately precipitation days 30 mm and snow cover days) are also made available in the service. The grids are divided into two future periods (2031-2060 and 2071-2100), each structured according to the climate scenarios RCP2.6, RCP4.5 and RCP8.5. The strength of the possible climate signal is divided according to the 15th, 50th and 85th scenarios. percentile. Averages (absolute, abs) and the change signal (delta change, diff) of 2031-2060 and 2071-2100 based on 1971-2000 are shown. Data source: German Weather Service (DWD). Further information from the German Weather Service must be observed: https://www.dwd.de/DE/service/rechtliche_hinweise/rechtliche_hinweise_node.html
