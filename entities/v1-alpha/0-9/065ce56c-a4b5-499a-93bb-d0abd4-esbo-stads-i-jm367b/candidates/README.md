# Esbo stads inköpsfakturor

> The data includes the City of Espoo's purchases of services, materials, supplies and goods, as well as rents and other expenses paid since 2012. The data includes the purchase invoice information of the City of Espoo's profit units, cost centres, commercial enterprises and funds. The data has been extracted from the city's accounting system at the invoice row level and the data has been supplemented with, for example, the name data of the organisation and account groups. The dataset contains the following column data: city (standard data), profit centre and cost centre named, booking date, invoice number, account group, account number, account name, supplier's name, supplier's business ID, invoice total in euros and order number, if any. Amounts are usually shown net of VAT. The data does not include purchases from private individuals or business names, grants paid to private individuals, etc. data covered by personal data protection. Information has been classified to facilitate the use of the material. The accounts are classified by account group as follows: purchases of services; materials, supplies and articles; rents; other expenses; capitalised expenditure and purchases of fixed assets. The classifications can be used to view purchases from the data, for example, by supplier and at the account level. The profit unit column contains the city's profit unit information or task area information for investments in the base city (for example, 911 General Administration and 941 Traffic Routes). The 2016 dataset has been prepared in accordance with the [national instructions for opening purchase invoices](https://www.avoindata.fi/data/fi/dataset/invoice opening instructions for municipalities).
