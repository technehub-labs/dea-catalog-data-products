# City of Vantaa revenue and expenditure

> The data includes the income and expenditure of the City of Vantaa according to the financial statements and the utilisation plans of the budgets for the years in question. The dataset includes the utilisation plan and implementation data of the city itself and its municipal enterprises and funds. The All- tab shows how the units (column B) are connected to the unit groups (column D), sectors (column F), divisions (column H) and companies (column J). In addition, this tab shows the links of the accounts (column L) to different account groups (column M). The data can be viewed down to the unit and account level. From 2020 onwards, the data only contains the data tab (All).
