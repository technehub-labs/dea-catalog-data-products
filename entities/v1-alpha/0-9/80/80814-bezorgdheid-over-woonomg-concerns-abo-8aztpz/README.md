# Concerns about the residential environment

> This table provides figures on safety of situations in the residential environment and conditions in the residential environment that can have harmful effects on health. This is the proportion of people aged 16 or over who are seriously concerned about different situations in the previous 12 months. The figures are based on the Research Experience Living Environment (OBW). CBS conducts this research in collaboration with the National Institute for Public Health and the Environment (RIVM).
