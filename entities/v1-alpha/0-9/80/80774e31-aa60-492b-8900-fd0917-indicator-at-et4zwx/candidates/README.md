# Indicator atlas: Population - mobility rate

> (also known as total mobility or migration volume) Sum of movers (main residence) of the corresponding category and movers (main residence) of the corresponding category per 1 000 main residents (mean main resident population in the calendar year) of the corresponding category. Both extra-urban (beyond the city limits of Munich) as well as inner-city residents and those who have moved away (= those who have moved within the urban area, also within the spatial unit) are taken into account. **Please note the data descriptions: [Information on the indicator atlas](https://opendata.muenchen.de/pages/indicator atlas).**
