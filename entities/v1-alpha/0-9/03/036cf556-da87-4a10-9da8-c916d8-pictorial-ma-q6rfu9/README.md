# Pictorial map of the City of Helsinki

> The photographic map is a bird's-eye view map of Helsinki's inner city. Buildings and other objects are presented in three dimensions. The map covers the southern inner city of Helsinki. The scale of the data is 1:6700. The data was last updated in March 2014. The data is currently not updated. **Preview of materials in the map.hel.fi service:** - [kartta.hel.fi](https://kartta.hel.fi/link/dAVEmc) **Code(s):** - ETRS-GK25 (EPSG:3879). Maintenance coordinate system. The data can also be projected to other coordinate systems.
