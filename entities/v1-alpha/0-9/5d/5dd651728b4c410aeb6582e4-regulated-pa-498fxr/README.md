# Regulated parking for people with reduced mobility in the Commune of Lyon

> The possibility of parking on roads or in car parks, public or private, helps to ensure the accessibility of motorized travel for people with disabilities and people with reduced mobility (PRM). A comprehensive legal framework exists to guarantee this right. On the one hand, there is free parking on all public spaces in VOIRIE and the right to use the spaces reserved for holders of a mobility card mentioning "parking". On the other hand, there are the obligations of communities and managers of institutions receiving the public to create accessible reserved spaces. This dataset presents the location of regulated locations for persons holding the "Persons with Reduced Mobility" card.
