# UDP - Personnel training on digital skills

> This indicator measures the share of tourism ecosystem enterprises providing ICT training to their personnel. It is based on data collected by the National Statistical Institutes through questionnaires to enterprises with 10 or more employees and self-employed persons (NACE sectors 55, 56, 79). The indicator is part of the digital pillar of the EU Tourism Dashboard. UNIT OF MEASURE: %. RESOLUTION: NUTS0. COMPLETENESS: FI missing. POLICY CONTEXT: It provides an indication of the level of investment of enterprises in integrating and upgrading their digital competencies. Higher values indicate higher shares of digital training. METHODOLOGY: The indicator is a weighted sum, calculated as follows: (percentage_ESTAT10_I55_56 * weightI55_56) + (percentage_ESTAT10_N79 * weightN79). Weight is the share of number of enterprises in the specific NOGA sector (I55+I56 or N79), over the total number of enterprises in NOGA sectors I55,I56,N79. Percentage is the NOGA specific share of enterprises providing personnel training on digital skills. UNCERTAINTY AND LIMITATIONS: Limited NACE breakdown. Not including micro enterprises (<10 employees). DATA SOURCES: isoc_ske_ittn2 (10_I55_56, 10_N79) [ESTAT]; sbs_na_sca_r2 [ESTAT].
