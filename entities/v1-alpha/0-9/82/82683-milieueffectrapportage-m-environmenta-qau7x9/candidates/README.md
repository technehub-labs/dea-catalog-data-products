# Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Energy Transition 2022 Geese Rest Areas

> Dataset for the Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Energy Transition 2022 adopted by the Provincial Executive on 4 October 2022. This is a plan-m.e.r. belonging to the Environmental Policy Review module Energy Transition that was adopted by Provinciale Staten on 14 December 2022. The aim of this EIA is to make the environmental effects of the integration of future development of wind and solar energy within the search areas from the Regional Energy Strategy (RES) 1.0 transparent so that they can be weighed when determining the Energy Transition module design policy. Contains the data for carrying out the environmental impact assessment on the criterion of geese resting areas.
