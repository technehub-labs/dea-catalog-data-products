# Vanda stads inköpsfakturor

> The data includes the City of Vantaa's purchases of services, materials, supplies and goods since 2012. The data includes the purchase invoice data of all the City of Vantaa's industries, non-financial corporations and funds. The data has been extracted from the city's accounting system at the invoice row level, and the data has been supplemented with, for example, the names of organisations and account groups, as well as the Y-identities of the suppliers. The dataset contains the following column data: company, profit area and cost centre, date of registration, receipt number, account group, account name, account number, supplier's name, business ID, invoice total in euros, order and partner company. The data has been classified to facilitate the use of the data. The organisation is classified according to companies: City of Vantaa, enterprise, fund and by profit area. Accounts are classified by account group as follows: purchases of services, personnel services, other expenses, acquisitions of fixed assets and materials, supplies and goods. The suppliers are classified according to the partnership into joint-stock companies and joint municipal authorities belonging to the urban group, as well as external suppliers. With the help of classifications, it is also possible to view purchases from the material, for example, by supplier and at the account level. The results area and cost location data are not complete, as completing the data would require a review of individual supporting documents. As of 2024, the data do not include data from private traders considered to be personal data within the meaning of the GDPR. The data is updated approximately once a month.
