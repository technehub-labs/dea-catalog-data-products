# Vantaa, Kerava and Järvenpää cities’ climate survey for businesses

> The data includes data from a business survey carried out jointly by the cities of Vantaa, Kerava and Järvenpää in 2021. The data includes responses from 91 companies or other organisations. The data was collected using the Webropol questionnaire from 1 to 21 November 2021 as part of the joint climate cooperation project between the cities of Vantaa, Kerava and Järvenpää. The questionnaire is available at [this link](https://www.hel.fi/hel2/datacentre/data/documents/_valinen_climate cooperation_query.pdf). The purpose of the survey was to find out companies' views on climate cooperation between the municipality and the business sector. In particular, the survey charted the hopes and expectations of companies towards the municipality. The aim was to gather information for the cities of Vantaa, Kerava and Järvenpää as a basis for developing climate cooperation. Answers that allow the respondents to be identified have been removed from the data. Open answers about the organisation, the industry, the location of the company and local cooperation have been removed from the data, as well as the last question about leaving contact information with the answers. The result compilation compiled from the results of the survey is available on the City of Vantaa's website: [link to the results](https://www.vantaa.fi/sites/default/files/document/Company%20climate survey%202021.pdf).
