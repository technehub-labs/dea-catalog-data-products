# Total quantity of plant protection product purchased in France

> The Ecophyto 2030 strategy is the national strategy to reduce dependency and risk associated with the use of plant protection products; the widespread use of these products has increased productivity but has negative consequences for human health and the environment as a whole (fauna, flora, aquatic environments and water resources). The indicator represents the quantities of phytosanitary substances purchased in France in the year, in tonnes. The data relate to purchases and sales of plant protection products, from the National Sales Bank (BNV-D) fed by distributors of plant protection products. They concern the quantities of active substances contained in plant protection products and do not take into account adjuvants. The quantities of active substances purchased do not necessarily reflect the place, quantity applied or period of application of the treatments.
