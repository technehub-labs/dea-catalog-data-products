# Education - colleges of the department of Haute-savoie - 2025

> Education - colleges of the department of Haute-Savoie - 2025 colleges-74 The colleges of the department of Haute-Savoie and their sectors. Public colleges give priority to students living in their recruiting sector. This sector is determined by the student’s place of residence. It is therefore neither the primary school attended nor the place of professional practice of the parents that determines the sector college.
