# PDM 2024 - Conditioning Plant - National Agricultural Reserve

> # 2aamendment to the 1st revision of the Municipal Master Plan The Municipal Master Plan of Águeda (PDM of Águeda) had its first version published in 1995 and during its validity was subject to corrections, amendments and a revision. To date, it is worth mentioning, as the most significant, the 1st Review of the MIP of Águeda (Notice No. 3341/2012, published in DR No. 44, 2nd series, 1 March), which repealed the MIP of 1995, and the 2nd Amendment to the 1st Review of the MIP of Águeda (Notice No. 3841/2024, published in DR No. 35, 2nd series, of 19 February). The elements of the PDM of Águeda are available in [Plano Diretor Municipal de Águeda - CM Águeda] (https://www.cm-agueda.pt/viver/urbanismo-ordenamento-do-territorio-e-politicas-de-habitação/planos-municipais-de-ordenamento-territorio/plano-diretor-municipal-de-agueda) The plants that make up the PDM of Águeda are as follows: Conditioning Plant – General Conditions; Conditioning Plant – Forest Fire Hazard; Conditioning Plant – Forest Fire Protection Network; Conditioning Plant – National Agricultural Reserve; Conditioning Plant – National Ecological Reserve; Spatial Planning Plan – Spatial Planning; Planning Plan – National Ecological Structure; Planning Plan – Heritage; Planning Plan – Flood and Flood Risk; Spatial Planning Plan – Clearance Areas; Planning Plan – Sensitive and Mixed Areas. The spatial data of the PDM of Águeda provided by the municipality do not exempt the consultation of the elements of the plan in the format published in the Diário da República. The scale of use of spatial data is 1/10 000 (plan publication scale).
