# Accessibility alert for the public transport network in Lyon

> From rolling stock renewal to the accessibility of stops, from passenger information to the implementation of specific services, SYTRAL Mobilités, in conjunction with its partners, has made the accessibility of its public transport networks a priority. Accessibility alerts identify elevators and escalators currently unavailable in metro and tram stations equipped with the Transport en Commun Lyonnais (TCL) network.
