# GLA Health Survey Infographics

> <dp-prose> <p>Over 660 <a href="http://talklondon.london.gov.uk/">Talk London</a> users took part in the GLA&apos;s online health survey during March 2014. This explored people&rsquo;s experiences of health services, views on health policies and health behaviours. An identical telephone poll with 1000 Londoners also ran between 14 and 16 March. The results will be used by the Mayor&rsquo;s Health Team and the London Health Commission to inform their recommendations on improving health in London.
