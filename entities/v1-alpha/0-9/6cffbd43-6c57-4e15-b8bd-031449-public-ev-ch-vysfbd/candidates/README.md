# Public EV Charging Points DLR

> Location of the EV (Electric Vehicle) charging points in DLR county. This data was collected by Compass in a survey undertaken in 2019 and may be missing charging points that have been installed since then. Certain restrictions may apply as some equipment may be on private property/or for customers only. For more information on EV Charging Points in DLR: https://www.dlrcoco.ie/travel-transport/parking/electric-vehicle-charging-points
