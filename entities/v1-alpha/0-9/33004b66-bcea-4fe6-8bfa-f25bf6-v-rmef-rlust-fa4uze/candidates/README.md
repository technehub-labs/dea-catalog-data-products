# Värmeförlusten i Helsingfors byggnader

> The material describes the heat loss from the roofs of Helsinki. Thermal radiation detected on the roofs of buildings is presented in 30 cm pixels. The roofs of Helsinki were photographed using a thermographic camera in March 2015. Thermal radiation depends on the shape and structure of the roof as well as the temperature of the room below, so it is not a direct indication of the quality of the roof insulation. The material includes an [interpretation key](https://avoidatastr.blob.core.windows.net/opendata/OpenData/9_Maps/Maps_thermal radiation/interpretation_avain.JPG) that can be used to determine what the colour of your roof means in terms of insulation quality, based on the properties of your roof. The data was completed in the Decumanus project and was produced by Eurosense, a Belgian geographic information services company participating in the project. **The material can be previewed and downloaded from HSY's map service:** - [Heat loss of buildings in Helsinki](https://kartta.hsy.fi/?zoomLevel=4&coord=25497952.5_6677018.5&mapLayers=108+100+raster&showMarker=true) **Coordinate systems:** - ETRS-GK25 (EPSG:3879). Maintenance coordinate system. The data can also be projected to other coordinates. Other possible coordinates can be found in the GetCapabilities document of the interface service. **API address:** - WMS interface: [https://kartta.hsy.fi/geoserver/wms](https://kartta.hsy.fi/geoserver/wms?request=getCapabilities) **Layers:** - climate_ja_energy:thermo_full_mosaic_helsinki
