# Agriculture; investments and stocks, national accounts

> This table contains data from the economic accounts for agriculture. The figures refer to gross investment and changes in inventories. Data available from: 1995 Status of figures: The years in the period 1995-2023 are final. The years 2024 and 2025 have provisional status. Changes as of 1 October 2026: Data from the estimate of provisional figures 2025 have been added to this table. When will there be new figures? After the end of the reporting year, the provisional figures will be published after approximately 6 months. After 18 months, the final figures are published at the same time as the national accounts are published. The very preliminary figures for the current year will be available in December. These data are determined by the Agricultural Economic Institute in consultation with Statistics Netherlands. An update of the very preliminary figures will take place in January.
