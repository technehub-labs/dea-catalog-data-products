# BLOND: Building-Level Office eNvironment Dataset

> The BLOND dataset: continuous energy measurements of a typical office environment at high sampling rates with common appliances and load profiles. We provide voltage and current readings for aggregated circuits and matching fully-labelled ground truth data (individual appliance measurements). The dataset contains 53 appliances (17 classes) in a 3-phase power grid. BLOND-50 contains 213 days of uninterrupted measurements sampled at 50 kSps (aggregate) and 6.4kSps (individual appliances). BLOND-250 consists of the same setup: 50 days, 250 kSps (aggregates), 50 kSps (individual appliances). These are the longest continuous measurements at such high sampling rates and fully-labelled ground truth we are aware of. Third-party funding: Alexander von Humboldt Foundation established by the government of the Federal Republic of Germany, Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy on the basis of a decision by the German Bundestag
