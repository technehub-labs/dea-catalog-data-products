# London's Night Time Economy by Borough and MSOA

> <dp-prose> <p>Number of workplaces and employees working in industry sectors that operate in the evening or night. The &quot;night time economy&quot; is defined as the following Standard Industrial Classification 2007 (SIC 2007) industries:</p> <ul> <li> <p>Cultural and leisure activities</p> </li> <li> <p>Activities which support night time cultural and leisure activities</p> </li> <li> <p>24-hour health and personal social services</p> </li> <li> <p>Activities which support wider social and economic activities</p> </li> </ul> <p>This data is provided at Borough and MSOA level.</p> <blockquote> <p><a href="/night-time-observatory/">This dataset is included in the Greater London Authority&apos;s Night Time Observatory. Click here to find out more.</a></p> </blockquote> </dp-prose>
