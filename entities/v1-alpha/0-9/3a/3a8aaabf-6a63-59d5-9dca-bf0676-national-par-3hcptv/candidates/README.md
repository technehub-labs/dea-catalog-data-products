# National Parks as Tourism Attractions

> Ensuring visitor satisfaction and at the same time protecting natural resources by minimising negative environmental influences by visitors are the main tasks of a national park. This paper explores how knowledge about load limit management and visitors can be used to ensure that these goals are met. An extensive literature review was carried out, existing theories were investigated and contemporary applied concepts were identified. Furthermore, expert interviews were conducted in five Austrian national parks to learn about their approach as well as about new and innovative concepts in national park management. Literature served as the basis for the interpretation of the research results. It is shown that although many procedures exist regarding the setting of load-bearing capacity limits as well as negative visitor influences, there are difficulties in implementing them globally. Correlations and interdependencies between management concepts are examined in the course of this thesis and it is explained how the knowledge about them can be applied in practice.
