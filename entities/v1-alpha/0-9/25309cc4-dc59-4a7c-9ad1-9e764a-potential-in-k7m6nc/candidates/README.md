# Potential intercropping areas before summering

> The surfaces are part of the field blocks of the nitrate backdrop. Within those areas, Paragraph 13a(2)(7) of the DüV provides that an intermediate crop is to be grown in autumn on agricultural land on which the long-term average annual precipitation is >= 550 mm and may not be broken down before 15 January if the subsequent crop is sown or planted after 1 February and fertilisers with a significant nitrogen content are to be applied before or for that crop. Areas on which the pre-cultivation took place after 1 October are exempted from this requirement. The designation of this site is linked to the nitrate site and is given only for the affected field blocks. The spatial data of the reference parcels concerned will be calculated annually on 1 February.
