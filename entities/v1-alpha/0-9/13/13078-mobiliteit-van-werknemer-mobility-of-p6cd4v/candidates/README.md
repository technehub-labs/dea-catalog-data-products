# Mobility of workers; Characteristics of mobility, 4th kw. 2016-3rd Q. 2018

> This table provides figures on the shares of different forms of inflows and outflows. The shares are a percentage of the total mobility of workers in the care and welfare sector without University Medical Centers (UMC). The reference date for the figures is the last day of each quarter. The figures are divided into different branches in the care and welfare sector. The figures can be broken down by region (part of the country, province, RegioPlus labour market region). Employees are counted as the number of unique individuals within an industry who accrue pension at the Pension Fund for Care and Welfare (PFZW). The figures in this table come from PFZW. UMCs do not participate in this pension fund. Data on these institutions is currently missing, so the totals for the care and welfare sector in this table are currently missing. This table was developed within the framework of the Labour Market, Care and Welfare (AZW) research programme. For more trends and developments in the field of the labour market in care and welfare, see www.AZWinfo.nl (see paragraph 3). Data available from: Q4 2016 to Q3 2018. Status of figures: The figures for the two most recent quarters in the table are very preliminary. All other figures are provisional. As this table has been discontinued, the data are no longer made definitive. Changes as of 22 July 2019: This table has been discontinued. When will there be new figures? No longer applicable. This table has been followed by the new tables (see paragraph 3).
