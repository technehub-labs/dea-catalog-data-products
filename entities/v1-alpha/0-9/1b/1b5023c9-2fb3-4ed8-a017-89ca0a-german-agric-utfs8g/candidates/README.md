# German Agricultural Open Data Platform for Food Security

> From the field of plant genetic resources, Germany provides data on national gene bank stocks. This includes the collections • the national ex situ gene bank of the Leibniz Institute for Plant Genetics and Crop Plant Research (IPK) with almost 150,000 accessions from over 3,000 different crop species, • the Deutsche Genbank fruit with over 1,000 additions to the fruit types apple, cherry and strawberry, • the Deutsche Genbank Vines with more than 4,400 accessions, • the Deutsche Genbank Ornamental Plants with over 12,000 accessions, including data on rhododendron and rose, • the Genetic Bank for Wild Plants for Food and Agriculture (WEL) with over 3,000 accessions of native wild plants, • the gene bank Tobacco with almost 800 accessions, • and information on current varieties for diversity provided by German breeders for the Multilateral System of the International Seed Treaty. In order to ensure efficient data exchange, the data fields comply with the internationally coordinated requirements of the EURISCO exchange format, which is essentially based on the "Multi-Crop Passport Descriptor List" of FAO and Bioversity International. The above collections and further information are provided in the National Inventory of Plant Genetic Resources in Germany (PGRDEU) at http://pgrdeu.genres.de/index.
