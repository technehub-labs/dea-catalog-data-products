# 1967 Local elections- election statistics

> This document sets out the following statistics from the 1967 local elections: i) The number of local electors, total poll, valid poll, percentage turnout, total invalid votes and invalid votes as percentage of total votes cast in elections in the County and County Boroughs Council elections. ii) The number of electors, valid poll and percentage poll for elections in the County Borough and Urban District Council elections. iii) The number of electors, valid poll and percentage poll for elections in the Town Commissioners elections. iv) The number of candidates and percentage of first-preference votes for each political party in County Council elections. v) The number of candidates and percentage of first-preference votes for each political party in County Borough Council elections. vi) The number of candidates and percentage of first-preference votes for each political party in County Borough and Urban District County elections. vii) The number of candidates and percentage of first-preference votes for each political party in Town Commissioners elections.
