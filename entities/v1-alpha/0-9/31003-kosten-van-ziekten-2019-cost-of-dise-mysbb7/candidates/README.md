# Cost of diseases 2019

> The figures in this table come from the Cost of Diseases study 2019 (KVZ) of the National Institute for Public Health and the Environment (RIVM), carried out in collaboration with the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS). This table was created for the RIVM website vzinfo.nl (information on public health and care) and contains data on healthcare expenditure in the Netherlands broken down by dimensions of care use (gender, age, diagnosis) and care provision (sector, care function and form of financing) for the year 2019. Data available from: 2019 Status of figures: Definitive. Changes as of 10-08-2022: None, this is a new table When will there be new figures? Every two years. The Cost of Diseases study itself comes out every four years, but for each intermediate two-year period, an estimated allocation based on demographic extrapolation takes place. Figures for a reference year shall become available two years after the end of that reference year. The next update will cover the year 2021 and will be published in 2023.
