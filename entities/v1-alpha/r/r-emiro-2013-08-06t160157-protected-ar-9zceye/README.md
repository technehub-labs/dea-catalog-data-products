# Protected Areas - Parks and Reserves (areas)

> The stratum represents the zoning of National and Regional Parks, Regional Nature Reserves and State Reserves, when the latter do not fall within already mapped Parks. The National Parks are represented only for the area falling within the territory of the Emilia-Romagna Region. The delimitation of the protected areas is defined at the time of the establishment of the Parks and Reserves and, for the Parks, it can be subsequently perfected or expanded during the drafting of the relevant Territorial Plans (TPPs). This information layer represents the zoning of the protected area as defined in the document corresponding to the most advanced stage of the territorial planning process of each individual protected area. The polygons, depending on the stage of the process, can refer to the maps defined in the Institutional Laws, adopted PTPs, approved PTPs, adopted PTP Variants, approved PTP Variants. For this reason, the database provides information on the reference planning act and the date of the same.Please note that until the planning process has reached a final outcome (= approval), not only the last acts but also any more restrictive rules defined in previous acts have value.
