# Natura 2000 network - SCI and SPA habitats

> Cartography for polygons: lines and points of habitats of Community interest (Annex I of Directive 92 143/EEC) within the Natura2000 Network Sites (SCIs and SPAs) of the Emilia-Romagna Region.The mapping of habitats, intended as a collection in a specific database of information on the recognition and location of natural environments, assumes the latter as the fundamental territorial units capable of containing and representing the extraordinary mosaic of biological diversity. The cartographic restitution that is obtained constitutes a first specific-thematic analysis within the perimeters of SCI and SPA, a sort of zoning within the Natura 2000 Network aimed at summarizing a complex and multifunctional information content. The final objective is a cognitive framework, as accurate and complete as possible, of the distribution of habitats and the constant and updated monitoring of evolutionary changes. It is understood that the Charter, by its nature of organized collection of all georeferenced information relating to habitats, is intended to illustrate and not to certify and has all the limits of an activity in continuous evolution and a simplified representation of reality. It is not a rule, but rather a valid, but not exhaustive, reference to the date of update. As such it will be of help to all those who want to deepen the territorial analysis, but it cannot replace the other cognitive activities aimed at planning and evaluating the impact of plans, projects and interventions on the areas of the Nature Network, whose realization provides, on a case-by-case basis, different scales of study of the phenomena.
