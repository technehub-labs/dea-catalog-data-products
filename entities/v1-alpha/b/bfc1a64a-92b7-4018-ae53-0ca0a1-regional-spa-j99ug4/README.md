# Regional spatial planning programme for the greater Braunschweig area 2008 - Agriculture - reserved area Agriculture (due to special functions of agriculture)

> This dataset contains the reserved areas of agriculture (due to special functions of agriculture) according to the drawing of the regional spatial planning programme for the greater Braunschweig area in the version of 2008. The statutes on the establishment of the Regional Spatial Planning Programme for the Greater Braunschweig Area 2008 were adopted by the Association Assembly of the Greater Braunschweig Area Association on 20 December 2007. Pursuant to Section 8(6) of the Lower Saxony Act on Spatial Planning and Regional Planning (NROG), as amended on 7 June 2007 (Nds. GVBl. 223), the Lower Saxony Ministry of Food, Agriculture, Consumer Protection and Regional Development - Government Representation Braunschweig - as the highest regional planning authority, has adopted the Regional Spatial Planning Programme 2008 for the greater Braunschweig area by decree of 30 April 2008 - ref.: RV BS 1.4-20303/ZGB2008 approved. The Regional Spatial Planning Programme 2008 for the greater Braunschweig area will enter into force on 1 June 2008. The Regional Spatial Planning Programme 2008 defines the intended spatial and structural development of the greater Braunschweig area. The district-free cities of Braunschweig, Salzgitter and Wolfsburg as well as the districts of Gifhorn, Goslar, Helmstedt, Peine and Wolfenbüttel belong to the association area of the greater Braunschweig area.
