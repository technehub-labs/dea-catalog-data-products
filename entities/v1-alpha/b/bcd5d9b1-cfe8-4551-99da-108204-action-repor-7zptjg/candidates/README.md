# Action report for a socially and climate-friendly mobility transition.

> In the report on measures for a socially and climate-friendly mobility transition on behalf of the Federal Ministry for Climate Protection, the Environment, Energy, Mobility, Innovation and Technology (BMK), the Federal Environment Agency examined 13 possible individual measures to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from the transport sector. In addition to the savings potential, the measures were subjected to a comprehensive acceptance analysis, in the context of which survey participants were to put together their preferred package of measures, among other things. Selected measures were also examined with regard to their macroeconomic effects, e.g. on value added and employment in Austria. The action report serves as a technical basis for national decision-making processes for the greening of the transport sector from an economic and social science point of view.
