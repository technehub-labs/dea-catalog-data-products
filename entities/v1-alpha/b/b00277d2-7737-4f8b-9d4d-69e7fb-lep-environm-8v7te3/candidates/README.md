# LEP Environment 2004 Priority Areas for Open Space Protection Saarland

> Extensive presentation of the priority areas for open space protection in the context of the state development plan for the environment. The priority areas for open space protection (VFS) serve the biotope network as well as the protection and preservation of contiguous uncut and unconstructed landscape components. The use of the VFS for residential, commercial or recreational buildings and the construction of wind turbines is not permitted. The data are based on definitions from the landscape framework plan, the species and biotope conservation programme and the opinion of an integrated open space and use concept for the Saartal.
