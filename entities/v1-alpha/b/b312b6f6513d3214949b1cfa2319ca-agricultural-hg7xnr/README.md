# Agricultural land renting prices

> Definition The farm rent is the amount that the tenant pays every year to the owner for the use of his land for agricultural purposes. -------- Farm rent prices are part of the annual survey on harvest estimates. Up to 2013, this survey was carried out by agricultural correspondents. From 2014 on, the survey switched to a postal survey conducted directly with farmers. The survey on harvest estimates and farm rents relies on a sample of about 1,600 agricultural holdings. This sample was drawn to ensure representativeness at regional, provincial and agricultural region level. This survey was conducted in January following the reference year. Also in 2014, the maximum threshold for detecting outliers in calculating rent prices was updated. Metadata Harvest estimate and farm rents in the agriculture.pdf
