# RSA76 Family Comp Homes benef 2016a21

> Composition of households receiving the Active Solidarity Income (RSA all schemes, i.e. general scheme + agricultural scheme), by family composition and per year, in Seine-Maritime from 31/12/2016 to 31/12/2021. This dataset is derived from data from the Caisse d’Allocations Familiales (CAF). Metadata [Link to metadata](https://seine-maritime.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/aa39d6e7e40047659a7e98439d4fec76/data) Additional resources * Data-caf site ([see link in metadata](https://seine-maritime.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/aa39d6e7e40047659a7e98439d4fec76/data)) The CAF open data site offers a page on RSA data at the national, departmental, inter-communal and municipal levels. This data can be downloaded in csv. format. * Website Public Service: [https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/N19775](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/N19775) The official website of the French administration offers a page dedicated to Active Solidarity Income (RSA), including, in addition to general information on the conditions of access to the RSA, forms to be downloaded, simulators and related teleservices * Website Data DREES: _<HTTPS://DATA.DREES.SOLIDARITES-SANTE.GOUV.FR/’_ DREES (Directorate of Research, Studies, Evaluation and Statistics) is a directorate of the central administration of the health and social ministries. Its open data site offers several datasets relating to the RSA and in particular departmental data on recipients of the RSA from January 2017 to December 2021 (“Monthly data on solidarity benefits”), downloadable in xlsx format.
