# Map of Health Areas of Castile and Leon

> Layer of Health Areas of Castilla y León.Cartographic representation of the Health Areas in which the health system of Primary Care of Castilla y León is organized, whose regulation is established by Decree 108/1991 and subsequent modifications.Map elaborated from the integration of the municipal areas in accordance with the organization and existing distribution of the health resources on the territory of the Community.Result 11 Health Areas (two areas in León and Valladolid and one per province in the rest).
