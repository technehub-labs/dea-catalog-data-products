# Filed (incoming), determined and pending cases and matters at Land and environment courts and at the Land and Environment Court of Appeal

> Number of cases and matters at the five Land and environment courts and at the Land and Environment Court of Appeal. The cases and matters are divided in case categories. Pending cases and matters are ongoing (open) cases or matters at a specific date. On May 2, 2011, Land and environment courts were established at five of the country's district courts and the Land and Environment Court of Appeal was established as a part of Svea Court of Appeal. Previously, there were special property courts and environment courts at certain district courts.
