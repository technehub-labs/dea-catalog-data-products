# exp occitanie zas 2019 no2 moyan

> **Genealogy within the meaning of Inspire**: Indicators of exceedance of limit values (40 µg/m³ as an annual average for NO2 and PM10 and 35 days above 50 µg/m³ for PM10)). These indicators are the number of inhabitants affected and the cumulative area. Data created with a model of atmospheric dispersion at the local scale for the AAGs and RAZs of the Occitania region and at the regional scale for the RAZ. Use of the emission inventory of the Occitanie region. Modeling carried out in accordance with the recommendations of the business reference of the Central Air Quality Monitoring Laboratory (LCSQA). Demographic data comes from the Magic pop repository.
