# High-frequency mental health surveillance

> Within the framework of the Mental Health Surveillance (MHS) at the Robert Koch Institute (RKI), time series consisting of three-month moving estimators and smoothing curves are calculated for a selection of indicators of mental health in adults based on survey data. As a result, developments in the mental health of the adult population in Germany are to be observed with as little delay as possible and, in particular, negative developments are to be detected at an early stage. This high-frequency surveillance was originally developed against the background of new information needs on the development of the mental health of the population in the COVID-19 pandemic.</p>
