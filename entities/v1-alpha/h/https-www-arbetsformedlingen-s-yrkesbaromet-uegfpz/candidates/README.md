# Yrkesbarometer

> The dataset shows current assessments of job opportunities and the recruitment situation in various professions, both at county level and nationally. In addition to a current picture, a forecast is also given of how the demand for labor in each profession is expected to develop over a five-year period. The occupational barometer is updated twice a year, every spring and autumn..
