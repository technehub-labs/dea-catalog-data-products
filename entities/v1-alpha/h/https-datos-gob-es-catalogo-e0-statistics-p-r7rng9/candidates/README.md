# Statistics Personnel Official 2023

> Statistics that are part of the Military Statistical Yearbook in compliance with the regulations of the Defense Statistical Plan (PLANESTADEF), are gathered in a single publication, where all the tables of each monographic statistic are collected. Provides information on administrative situations, categories of public employment, scales and military jobs; distributions by province, gender, etc. The quality of the information contained in this work is based both on the processes of purification and correction to which the data were submitted, when the corresponding statistics were elaborated, and on the accuracy with which these disaggregated data were provided by the bodies depositing the information, this accuracy being a fundamental premise that defines, in general, the quality of any statistical information. The collaboration of the agencies that have provided the data, essential for the publication of this work, is appreciated. Likewise, the Statistics Unit of the Central Organ of the Ministry of Defense is open to all kinds of suggestions that contribute to improving and completing the published information.
