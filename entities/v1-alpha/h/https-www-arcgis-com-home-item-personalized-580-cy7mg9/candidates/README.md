# Personalized Social Support Measure 1 Canton76 2017a2023 20240729

> This dataset presents the number of annual beneficiaries of a MASP1 (personalised social support and budget management assistance with receipt and management of social benefits by the beneficiary) by canton of Seine-Maritime, per year, from 2017 to 2023. The visualization highlights the years with the highest number of beneficiaries per canton. The dataset contains empty boxes corresponding to the data concerned by statistical confidentiality (fewer than five beneficiaries in the canton in the year in question).
