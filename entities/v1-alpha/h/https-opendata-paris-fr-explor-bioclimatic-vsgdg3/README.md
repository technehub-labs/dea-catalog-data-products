# Bioclimatic PLU - Sites for the protection of social and solidarity economy activities

> Plan Local d'Urbanisme Bioclimatique de Paris - **Sites for the protection of social and solidarity economy activities** Version of the PLU voted by the Paris Council on 20 November 2024. Full documentation: See "Site for the protection of social and solidarity economy activities.pdf" attached. Data available for information and without regulatory value. To access the official PLU, consult the [Portal of Urban Planning Rules](https://regles-urbanisme.paris.fr/) of Paris. For other PLU data available on Opendata, see [this page](https://opendata.paris.fr/explore/?sort=modified&refine.keyword=PLU).
