# Lithuanian National Atlas (Volume III)

> Volume III of the National Atlas of Lithuania reflects political, economic, social, cultural and historical changes that occurred at the turn of the 20th-21st centuries. The Atlas consists of six large chapters that illustrate the development of the administrative division at the end of the 20th century — the beginning of the 21st century; dynamics of population, its national composition, professed religions and prevailing political attitudes; social situation of the national population and its development in recent decades; economic situation of the country, development of its agriculture and forestry; excavation of mineral resources and developed industries; the national transport system, transport of cargo and passengers, forms and volumes of communications services.
