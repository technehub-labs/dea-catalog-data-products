# Recovery plan, projects selected for the energy renovation of State buildings

> The recovery plan announced by the government on 3 September 2020 includes an extensive programme for the energy renovation of public buildings (of the state and its operators). These investments will make it possible both to support the construction sector by generating at local level multiple construction sites benefiting the entire fabric of construction companies, and to reduce the energy footprint of public buildings by proposing a rapid and significant action on energy consumption. This dataset allows you to list and visualize selected projects at regional, departmental and municipal level. For details: * The initial page on the EID website: <https://immobilier-etat.gouv.fr/les-grands-dossiers/france-relance-projects-renovation-energetique-batiments-publics/liste-projects-retained> * The scoreboard: <http://cartographie-plan-de-relance.portail-die.fr/batiments/>
