# Personalized Social Support Measure 2 Cantons76 2017a2023 20240729

> This dataset presents the number of annual beneficiaries of a MASP1 (personalised social support and budget management assistance with the collection and management of social benefits on behalf of the beneficiary) by canton of Seine-Maritime, per year, from 2017 to 2023. The visualization shows the number of beneficiaries per year and per canton. Data covered by statistical confidentiality (less than 5 beneficiaries in the canton in the year concerned) are indicated by the entry "S".
