# CAPB – Innovation Agriculture Award 2018 to 2021

> The Community of agglomeration Basque Country supports farmers in the implementation of innovative projects for the territory CAPB \- Innovation agriculture award 2018-2019-2020 The Basque Country Agglomeration Community wishes to make income improvement a fundamental challenge for Basque agriculture and fishing. It also wants to help and support agriculture in the Basque Country to meet current ecological and environmental challenges, while meeting the challenge of a sustainable economic model. To achieve these objectives, the Community of Aglomeration has a strong desire to support innovation and experimentation on these issues and the emergence of reproducible projects throughout the territory. In addition to the other schemes put in place, three Calls for Projects on the agricultural and agri-food sector were launched in 2018 and renewed in 2019 and 2020. PAAs: Innovative and experimental farms since 2018 New sector since 2018 New products since 2018 New modes of commercialisation in short circuit and proximity since 2020
