# UTAS CD76 2022

> Limits and names of the Territorial Units of Social Action (UTAS) of Seine-Maritime, elaborated from the data of the Department of Seine-Maritime and the IRIS perimeters of the IGN (IGN_n _irisge _s_d76). The UTAS, organised at headquarters and in places where the public is welcomed, are responsible for steering and organising social action on a territorial basis in connection with diagnosis and the local social project. They contribute to the declination of departmental schemes on the territory and implement the global and socio-professional social support as well as the support of specific audiences. This file, used with a number of social data available on this site (accessible by a search on the tag "UTAS"), makes it possible to better visualize the perimeters to which this data applies. To view the scopes of the UTAS on the same map as the data concerned, follow the methodology proposed under the "Online Help" tab.
