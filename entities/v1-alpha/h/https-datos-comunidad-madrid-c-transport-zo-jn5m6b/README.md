# Transport zoning ZT84 of the EDM2018 and ESM2014

> Transport zones (TZs) constitute the basic spatial unit for the analysis and aggregation of information. The zoning of the EDM 2018 and ESM 2014, is constituted by 84 transport zones for the scope of the Community of Madrid. The ZT84 has a scale of territorial division at the level of districts and municipalities, except in crown C in which aggregations of several municipalities are given.rrFor technical reasons, the date of last update of the data is not automatically refreshed, but is specified in the destination web address.
