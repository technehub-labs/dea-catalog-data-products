# Contact - Administrative Departments of the Municipality of Blato

> The administrative bodies of the Municipality of Blato are established for the performance of tasks falling within the self-governing scope of the Municipality of Blato as a unit of local self-government and state administration tasks that have been transferred to the Municipality of Blato. The administrative bodies shall be composed of the following administrative departments: MANAGEMENT SECTION FOR GENERAL AFFAIRS, GENERAL ASSESSMENT MANAGEMENT AND SOCIAL ACTIONS MANAGEMENT DEPARTMENT FOR BUDGET, FINANCIAL, ECONOMIC AND PREPARATORY OF EU FUNDS PROJECTS MANAGEMENT SECTION FOR COMMUNICAL ACTIONS, INFRASTRUCTURE, ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AND ECONOMIC PROTECTION
