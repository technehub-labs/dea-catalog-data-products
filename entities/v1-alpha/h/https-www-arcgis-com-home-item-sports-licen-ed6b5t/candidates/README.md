# Sports licenses by federation and municipality of Seine-Maritime in 2018

> This dashboard allows to dynamically visualise the information relating to holders of a sports license in Seine-Maritime: list of federations, number of licensees per federation, by municipality and sex, location of licensees by federation, number of sports licence holders per municipality and sex. This data, geolocated to the municipality, comes from the census of licences and clubs among sports federations approved by the Ministry in charge of sports. It is a census at the place of habitation of the person and not at the place of practice. Data are not available for all federations. A number of them did not have fully geo-localizable data in the municipality allowing for exhaustive exploitation.For example, FF data for canoe-kayak (1125 licensed in Seine-Maritime in 2019), fencing (970 licensed in Seine-Maritime in 2019), etc. Metadata Link to metadata: [first](https://seine-maritime.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/f63aa2fb9dcb4c81bcd0c19218fc4fcf/data) and [second](https://seine-maritime.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/82817495e5ae47c0b006cf9565f32761/data) source Additional resources * Website of the National Institute of Youth and Popular Education: [https://injep.fr/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/licences-par-departement-2019.xlsx](https://injep.fr/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/licences-par-departement-2019.xlsx) This INJEP website, which is a nationally competent service of the Ministry of National Education, Youth and Sports, offers a lot of data related to sport, including licenses by Department in 2019 * Website of the Ministry of Sports: <https://www.sports.gouv.fr/organisation/publications/statistiques/chiffres-cles/>(https://www.sports.gouv.fr/organisation/publications/statistiques/chiffres-cles/) The Ministry’s website offers various publications or downloadable data (key figures, detailed data since 2000) and information on the methodology of the census of sports licences and clubs. * INSEE website: [https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques
