# Tourism: agenda of activities

> Agenda of tourist activities of the demarcation of Barcelona, in which filters can be applied by region, cities and towns, typology of activity and start and end date. Includes images, location, labels and category of each activity. The Diputación de Barcelona, through the brand "Barcelona és molt més", promotes the social and economic development of the municipalities of the province of Barcelona through the tourism sector, monitoring to maintain a harmonious balance between this development and its sociocultural and environmental environments in which it develops.
