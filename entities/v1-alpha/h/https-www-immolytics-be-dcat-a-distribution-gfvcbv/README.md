# Distribution of EPC energy classes by Belgian municipality

> Share of homes in each of the seven energy classes, A to G, in percent. One row per municipality, keyed by NIS code. Wallonia (262 municipalities): breakdown specific to each municipality, from registered EPC certificates (ODWB, SPW Énergie). Brussels: not included, as Brussels Environment publishes only three figures for the whole Region (share A or B, share E or better, share G) and no A to G breakdown. Flanders: not included, as no breakdown by municipality is published as open data.
