# Air Health NO2 Index

> Municipal indicator to make the link between air quality and health impacts. Indicator calculated over 3 years from modeled fine scale annual average nitrogen dioxide (NO2) data. Nitrogen dioxide (NO2) is emitted during combustion phenomena, mainly by combining nitrogen and oxygen from the air. The main sources are vehicles and combustion plants.
