# WFD River, Canal, and SWT Water Bodies Cycle 4 Classification 2025 (Full Resolution)

> This is a polyline dataset showing Water Framework Directive (WFD) River, Canal, and Surface Water Transfer Water Bodies. River centrelines represent the official river water body definition for the WFD. This dataset provides attribution on the 2025 classification results, as well as other relevant information and data about each river, canal, and surface water transfer water body.
