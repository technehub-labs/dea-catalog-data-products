# INSPIRE HH Transport Networks ATKIS Basic DLM

> In this dataset, the Hamburg transport networks are: Road, rail, air and water from the ATKIS base DLM source model included and presented in the INSPIRE target model. ATKIS® stands for Official Topographic-Cartographic Information System and was conceived as a joint project of the Association of Surveying Administrations of the Länder of the Federal Republic of Germany (AdV).
