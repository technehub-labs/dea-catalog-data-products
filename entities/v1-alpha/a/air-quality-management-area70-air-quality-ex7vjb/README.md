# Air Quality Management Area

> Line data displaying the location of designated St Helens Air Quality Management Areas (AQMA). St Helens Council are obliged to designate AQMA's to help improve air quality and the local environment. Officers from the council’s Environment Health Team monitor a number of air quality pollutants. The AQMA reference and name are within the table fields
