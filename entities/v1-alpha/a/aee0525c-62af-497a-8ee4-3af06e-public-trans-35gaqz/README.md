# Public transport quality classes in the German counties and municipalities 2025

> Public transport in Germany is part of public services and a prerequisite for getting to school or work, doing shopping, visiting doctors, visiting the theatre, meeting friends and relatives. A good public transport offer helps to establish equal opportunities for all population groups and to approximate the living conditions in the city and country. This analysis provides a Germany-wide overview of the quality of public transport. At the level of the districts and municipalities, it analyses how many people have access to public transport of a defined quality class (A-F). For the present evaluation, the transport planning company Plan4Better has analysed the timetable data for the year 2025 on behalf of Greenpeace.
