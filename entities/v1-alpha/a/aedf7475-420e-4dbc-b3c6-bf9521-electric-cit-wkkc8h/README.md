# Electric City Rüsselsheim - Mobility behavior, acceptance of electric cars and charging station configuration

> The data are part of the project "Electric City Rüsselsheim", which was funded by the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Protection (BMWK) as part of the "Immediate Clean Air Programme 2017-2020" (funding code: 01MZ18008B).From January to December 2020, RheinMain University of Applied Sciences conducted computer-based personal interviews (CAPI) with citizens from Rüsselsheim am Main and the surrounding area (n=466 households surveyed). The following topics were covered in the approximately 60-minute interview and are available in corresponding (individual) data sets (see documentation):- **Information on the household surveyed** (Socioeconomics, traffic situation)- **Information on all persons living in the household** (Sociodemography, mobility behaviour)- **Detailed information on all mobility tools available in the household** - Passenger cars - Motorcycles - Subscriptions for public transport (ÖV)- **Social psychological constructs:** Attitudes to and perception of electric cars, as well as environmental awareness- **Stated Adaptation Experiment:** Adaptation of mobility tools in response to hypothetical price regulations on fuel, electricity, public transport and purchase premiums for an e-car-Stated Preferences Experiment:** Preferences for charging station configuration
