# Apprenticeship Success Rates in England 2010/11

> Apprenticeship Success Rates for institutions in England for the academic year 2010/11. The data shows success rates, retention rate and achievement rates by type of provision and characteristics of the learners. It also shows success rates for each individual organisation. These datasets are also known as Apprenticeship National Success Rate Tables or Apprenticeship NSRTs.
