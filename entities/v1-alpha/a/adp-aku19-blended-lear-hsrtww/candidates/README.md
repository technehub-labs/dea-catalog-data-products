# Blended learning analyst in higher education, 2019

> Blended learning, which represents the intertwining of traditional teaching in a lecture room with implementation in an online environment, is becoming increasingly present in higher education. Learning management systems in which e-learning takes place store each student's interaction with the system in log files. At the same time, various databases of educational data are kept about the student in electronic form, and surveys are also increasingly implemented in the online environment. Analyzing and mining educational data collected in various databases in order to understand the learning process and extract information that would help in meaningful Decisions to improve the educational process, represents a scientific area called learning analytics. The aim of the research in the field of educational data was to improve Understanding the learning process in blended learning in higher education with the aim of identifying factors that have a significant impact on the student's work in the online classroom and during the activities Identify those that can be linked to final performance. The empirical part presents two common approaches of learning analytics performed with freely available software tools. The calculations are based on various databases of students of the Faculty of Administration. By mining educational data, we explored the predictive performance of various models predicting the final performance of the student on the basis of his activity in the online classroom and predispositions (learning approach, previous performance).
