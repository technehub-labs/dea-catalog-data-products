# Waste collection Groningen and Ten Boer

> The municipality of Groningen wants to investigate whether and how more waste can be separated and reused. Research and Statistics Groningen has prepared an online survey for this purpose. In the survey we asked questions about how and whether residents of Groningen and Ten Boer currently separate waste and what they think of waste collection. We also presented a number of future scenarios to the respondents. A number of open questions have been asked, and many opportunities have been given to provide explanations.
