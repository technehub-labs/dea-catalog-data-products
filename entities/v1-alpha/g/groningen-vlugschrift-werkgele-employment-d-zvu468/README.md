# Employment development

> The leaflets describe the development of employment and the number of branches in the municipality of Groningen. The data come from the annual employment survey of the Groningen Register of Establishments for Research and Statistics. Data is collected from all locations. The data are updated retroactively and are therefore not comparable to those described in previous versions.
