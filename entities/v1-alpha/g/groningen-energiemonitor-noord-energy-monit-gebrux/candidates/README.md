# Energy monitor Northern Netherlands

> The 4th edition of the Energy Monitor Northern Netherlands provides facts & figures from 2013 about the energy sector in the Energy Valley region: Drenthe, Fryslân, Groningen and Noord-Holland Noord. The monitor was commissioned by the Energy Valley Foundation and the northern provinces to make the regional energy developments visible. This 4th edition of the Energy Monitor Northern Netherlands has been designed in such a way that it can also be used to make the progress of the implementation of the Northern Energy Agenda SWITCH transparent.
