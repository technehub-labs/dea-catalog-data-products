# Civic education in schools

> Since the school year 2016/2017, the City of Rijeka has been providing pupils of higher grades of primary school with the opportunity to attend the courses of Civic Education. For this program, the City of Rijeka has also developed a handbook for students. Civic education is carried out according to the model of the extremely successful programme "My Rijeka", as an extracurricular activity.More on the programme Civic education https://www.rijeka.hr/themes-for-citizens/education-and-education/basic-schools/programs-and-projects-in-basic-schools/citizen-education/
