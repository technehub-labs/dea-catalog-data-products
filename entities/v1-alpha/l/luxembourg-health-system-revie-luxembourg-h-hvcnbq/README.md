# Luxembourg: Health system review

> The National Health Observatory, in collaboration with the European Observatory on Health Systems and Policies, published the Health System Review for Luxembourg in the Health Systems in Transition (HiT) series. This report provides a detailed analysis of the health system, its current and developing structures, reforms and policies. This report is the result of a valuable collaboration with many experts in the Luxembourg health system, whose invaluable support, information, availability, critical comments and proofreading were essential to its realization. This publication was presented on 5 February 2025 at a conference attended by Martine Deprez, Minister of Health and Social Security and many stakeholders in the health system. The conference was followed by a panel discussion on the challenges of sustainable health system resource planning. The report presents a comprehensive assessment of the Luxembourg health system, an essential resource for decision-makers, researchers and health professionals wishing to understand the challenges and dynamics of the Luxembourg health system. A concise version of the report is also available in French and English, summarizing the key elements of the health system in a clear and relevant way for authorities and the public.
