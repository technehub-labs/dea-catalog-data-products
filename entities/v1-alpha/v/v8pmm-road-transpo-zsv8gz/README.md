# Road Transport Energy Consumption, Borough

> <dp-prose> <p>Estimates of road transport consumption statistics for fuels used in road transport.</p> <p>(1) Personal travel includes buses, diesel cars, petrol cars and motorcycles.</p> <p>(2) Freight includes HGV, diesel LGV and petrol LGV.</p> <p>The UK total excludes motorcycles in Northern Ireland.</p> <p>There were major revisions to this data due to improvements in methodology, for more details please see the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/regional-energy-data-guidance-note">General Guidance Note</a>.</p> <p>2002-04 are experimental statistics. Later years are National Statistics. Therefore data created using differing methodologies should not be directly compared.</p> <p>Data for Middle Super Output Areas (MSOA) available.</p> <p>See more on <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/road-transport-consumption-at-regional-and-local-level">Sub-national road transport data</a> or <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/road-transport-energy-consumption-at-regional-and-local-authority-level">Road transport energy consumption at regional and local authority level</a>.</p> </dp-prose>
