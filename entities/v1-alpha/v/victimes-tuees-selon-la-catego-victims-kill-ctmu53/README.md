# Victims killed by category of road users 1991 – 2015

> This table gives for the years 1991-2015 the number of pedestrians, cyclists, mopeds, motorcyclists, 4-wheel drivers and 4-wheel or 2-wheel passengers killed as a result of a road traffic accident and is calculated annually by STATEC. The following definitions have been used: A road traffic accident is any unexpected and sudden event that occurred to one or more users on a lane normally open to traffic and that caused bodily injury provided that a moving vehicle was involved in the event. For statistics on bodily injuries only road traffic injuries reported to the Grand Ducal Police are taken into account. The data are drawn from the minutes of law enforcement officers. The victims killed include those killed instantly or who died as a result of the accident within 30 days. For more details: http://www.statistiques.public.lu/fr/méthodologie/méthodes/conditions-sociales/Accidents/accidents/index.html
