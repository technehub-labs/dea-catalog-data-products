# Survey on Educational-Training Transition and Labor Market Insertion (ETEFIL_AESO_2019). Students who left compulsory secondary education (ESO) in 2013-14. Restricted access rights.

> Confidential dataset for scientific purposes from the Survey on Educational-Training Transition and Labor Market Insertion (ETEFIL_AESO_2019) regarding students who left compulsory secondary education (ESO) in 2013-14. The survey aims to provide information on the subsequent educational or training path of these individuals, as well as various aspects of their labor market insertion process—namely, the transition from early school leaving or compulsory education to the labor market.
