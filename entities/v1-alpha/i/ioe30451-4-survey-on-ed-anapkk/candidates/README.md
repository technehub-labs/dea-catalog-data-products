# Survey on Educational-Training Transition and Labor Market Insertion (ETEFIL_GS_2019). Advanced Vocational Training graduates in 2013-14. Restricted access rights.

> Confidential dataset for scientific purposes from the Survey on Educational-Training Transition and Labor Market Insertion (ETEFIL_GS_2019) regarding Advanced Vocational Training graduates in 2013-14. The survey aims to provide information on the subsequent educational path of individuals who have completed these advanced vocational training studies, as well as various aspects of their labor market insertion process—namely, the transition from non-university higher education to the labor market.
