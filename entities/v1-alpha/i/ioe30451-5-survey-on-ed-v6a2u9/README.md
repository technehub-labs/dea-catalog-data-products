# Survey on Educational-Training Transition and Labor Market Insertion (ETEFIL_ESO_2019). Students who successfully completed compulsory secondary education (ESO) in 2013-14. Restricted access rights.

> Confidential dataset for scientific purposes from the Survey on Educational-Training Transition and Labor Market Insertion (ETEFIL_ESO_2019) regarding students who successfully completed compulsory secondary education (ESO) in 2013-14. The survey aims to provide information on the subsequent educational path of individuals who have completed secondary education and vocational training, as well as various aspects of their labour market insertion process—namely, the transition from education and training to the labour market.
