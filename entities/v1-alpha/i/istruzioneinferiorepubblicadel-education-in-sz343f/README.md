# Education Inferior Public of the city of Matera A.S. 2015-2016

> The dataset contains the number of pupils of the Public Infant, Primary and Lower Middle Schools grouped by classes and school complexes with their addresses and geographical coordinates. It is also necessary to specify that the data of the Secondary School of First Degree "Nicola Festa" of Via Lanera n.59 also includes three classes for a total of 61 students (one first, a second and a third) who for logistical reasons are located at the plexus of Via Francesco D'Alessio (commonly known as the school of Via Antonio Cappelluti).
