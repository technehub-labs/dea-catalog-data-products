# Intensity of urban traffic

> As part of the SUMBooST project, the City of Rijeka together with the Faculty of Transport and Traffic Sciences and Ericsson Nikola Tesla launched a project to improve urban traffic. The aim is to identify potential urban mobility measures that would encourage a shift to sustainable forms of transport between problematic transport zones, such as rail, public transport of buses, cable cars, classic or electric bicycles. These are the first results of analyses, and they show car, public city and total traffic by zones and by hours. https://www.rijeka.hr/gradska-uprava/eu-projekti/završeni-projekti/sumboost/
