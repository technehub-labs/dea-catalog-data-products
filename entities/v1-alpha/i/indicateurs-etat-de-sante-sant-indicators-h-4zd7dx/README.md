# Indicators - Health status - Perceived health

> This section looks at indicators of **perceived health**. The health status of a population reflects the general health of a group of people living in a given environment or sharing common characteristics, such as age, gender or other socio-demographic factors. Its assessment is based on a variety of indicators. By analysing these indicators and understanding the factors that influence them, policymakers, health and care institutions, health professionals and other stakeholders can develop policies and interventions to improve the health and well-being of the population as a whole.
